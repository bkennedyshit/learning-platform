# APP-08 — Music Theory

*Part of [[Bill's Vault/05-Knowledge_Foundation/10 - Learning App Material/README|10 - Learning App Material]]*

> **App context:** Music Theory is distinct from Track 35 (Music Production & Sound Design) — that track is about making music with software; this track is about the formal language music is written in. They complement each other: Music Theory gives vocabulary and grammar; Music Production gives the tools. AP Music Theory is one of the harder AP exams and highly rewarding for musically literate students.

## 3. Chapter Outline

| # | Chapter | Core skill |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 08.1 | **Notation & Rhythm** | Staff, clefs (treble, bass, alto, tenor); ledger lines; note names (all octaves); whole/half/quarter/eighth/sixteenth notes and rests; dots and ties; time signatures (simple: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4; compound: 6/8, 9/8, 12/8); beaming; tempo markings |
| 08.2 | **Scales & Key Signatures** | Major scales (construction: W-W-H-W-W-W-H); all 12 major key signatures (circle of fifths); natural minor scales (relative and parallel minor); harmonic and melodic minor; pentatonic and blues scales; modes (Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Locrian) |
| 08.3 | **Intervals** | Interval identification by number (unison through octave + compound intervals); interval quality (perfect, major, minor, augmented, diminished); enharmonic equivalents; interval inversion; consonance vs. dissonance |
| 08.4 | **Chords & Triads** | Triad construction (major, minor, augmented, diminished) in all positions (root, first inversion, second inversion); seventh chords (MM7, Mm7/dominant 7, mm7, dm7, °7); chord symbols (lead sheet vs. Roman numeral analysis) |
| 08.5 | **Harmony & Voice Leading** | Roman numeral analysis (I–VII in major and minor); functional harmony (tonic, predominant, dominant); cadences (authentic, half, plagal, deceptive); voice leading principles (SATB — soprano, alto, tenor, bass); parallel fifths and octaves |
| 08.6 | **Melodic Analysis & Composition** | Phrase structure (antecedent/consequent); motives and sequences; melodic contour; melodic composition techniques; harmonizing a melody with basic chord progressions; figured bass |
| 08.7 | **Form & Analysis** | Binary (AB) and ternary (ABA) form; rondo; theme and variations; sonata-allegro form; 12-bar blues form; analyzing complete works by structure; musical style periods (Baroque, Classical, Romantic, 20th century) |
| 08.8 | **Ear Training & Sight-Singing** | Melodic dictation; harmonic dictation; rhythmic dictation; sight-singing in solfège (fixed vs. moveable do); interval recognition by ear; chord quality identification; the AP free-response section (written melody harmonization, bass line, figured bass realization) |

## 4. App-Specific Feature: Audio Playback in Drills

Music Theory is the one subject where drills **must have audio**. The platform needs:
- MIDI playback of any chord/interval/melody in the drill
- Record-and-compare for sight-singing exercises
- Staff notation rendering (VexFlow — free JS library) for notation drills
