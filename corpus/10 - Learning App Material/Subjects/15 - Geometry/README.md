## 📚 Authoritative Catalogs & Sources

_Pulled verbatim from `Subject_Plan.md §2 — Free Learning Catalog`. Source of truth is the Subject_Plan; this section is a convenience copy._

- **[Khan Academy Geometry](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/geometry)** — complete free course; best for worked examples and practice
- **[GeoGebra](https://www.geogebra.org/geometry)** — free dynamic geometry tool; the most important software for this course (construct, drag, discover)
- **[OpenStax Geometry (2023)](https://openstax.org/details/books/contemporary-mathematics)** — free college-level text with geometry chapters
- **[Math Antics — Geometry (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@mathantics)** — clear visual explanations
- **[Crash Course Geometry (YouTube — various)](https://www.youtube.com)** — multiple channels; search "geometry proofs explained"
- **[Art of Problem Solving Introduction to Geometry](https://artofproblemsolving.com/store/item/intro-geometry)** — rigorous problem-based approach
- **[Euclid's Elements (free, Oliver Byrne visual edition)](https://www.c82.net/euclid/)** — the original geometry textbook, beautifully visualized
