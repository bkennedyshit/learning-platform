## 📚 Authoritative Catalogs & Sources

_Pulled verbatim from `Subject_Plan.md §2 — Free Learning Catalog`. Source of truth is the Subject_Plan; this section is a convenience copy._

- **[American Yawp (free US History textbook)](https://www.americanyawp.com/)** — peer-reviewed, free, college-level; the primary text ← **use this**
- **[Heimler's History APUSH (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@heimlershistory)** — the best AP US History channel; organized by exam unit
- **[Crash Course US History (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@crashcourse)** — John Green's 48-episode series; excellent engagement
- **[Khan Academy AP US History](https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/ap-us-history)** — exam-aligned practice
- **[Library of Congress Primary Sources](https://www.loc.gov/programs/teachers/)** — the definitive free primary source collection
- **[The Avalon Project (Yale)](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/subject_menus/major.asp)** — free constitutional and legal documents from all US eras
- **[Digital History](https://www.digitalhistory.uh.edu/)** — free narrative, primary sources, and images organized by era
- **[DBQ Project](https://www.dbqproject.com/)** — document-based question practice (free samples)
- **[Gilder Lehrman Institute](https://www.gilderlehrman.org/)** — free AP history resources; primary source documents
