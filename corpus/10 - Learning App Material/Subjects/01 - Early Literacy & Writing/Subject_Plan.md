# APP-01 — Early Literacy & Writing

*Part of [[Bill's Vault/05-Knowledge_Foundation/10 - Learning App Material/README|10 - Learning App Material]] · [[Bill's Vault/05-Knowledge_Foundation/10 - Learning App Material/K-12 Curriculum Pipeline|K-12 Curriculum Pipeline]]*

> **App context:** This track is built for the platform's K-5 learner segment. Every other subject in K-5 depends on reading fluency. This is the highest-leverage curriculum investment in the entire K-12 pipeline.

---

## 1. What This Track Is

Systematic phonics + reading comprehension + writing mechanics for grades K through 5. Built on the Science of Reading consensus: explicit, systematic phonics instruction is the most evidence-based approach to reading acquisition — not whole-language, not "balanced literacy."

**The two phases:**
- **K-2 (Decoding phase):** Phonemic awareness → letter-sound mapping → blending → fluency. If a child can't decode reliably by end of 2nd grade, every subsequent subject suffers.
- **3-5 (Comprehension & composition phase):** Vocabulary, text structure, inference, summarizing, paragraph writing, multi-paragraph essays.

## 3. Chapter Outline

| # | Chapter | Core skill | Grade band |
|---|---------|-----------|-----------|
| 01.1 | **Phonemic Awareness & the Alphabet** | Phoneme isolation, blending, segmenting (oral only — no letters yet); letter names and sounds (all 26); CVC word blending (*cat, dog, sit*); sight words — Dolch pre-primer (40 words) | K |
| 01.2 | **Phonics — Blending & Decoding** | Short vowel patterns (CVC); consonant blends (bl, cr, st, etc.); digraphs (sh, ch, th, wh, ck); long vowels — CVCe (*cake, bike, hope*); sight words Dolch primer + 1st grade | K-1 |
| 01.3 | **Fluency & Word Recognition** | Long vowel teams (ai/ay, ee/ea, oa, ue); r-controlled vowels (ar, er, ir, or, ur); diphthongs (oi/oy, ou/ow); reading rate targets (60-80 WCPM by end of grade 2); Dolch 2nd grade | 1-2 |
| 01.4 | **Vocabulary & Word Study** | Prefixes (un-, re-, pre-, mis-); suffixes (-ful, -less, -tion, -ness, -er); compound words; context clues; multiple-meaning words; Tier 2 academic vocabulary; Greek/Latin roots intro (bio, geo, graph) | 3-4 |
| 01.5 | **Reading Comprehension — Narrative Text** | Story elements (character, setting, plot, conflict, resolution); theme vs. main idea; character motivation and change; point of view; making inferences; text evidence; comparing and contrasting across stories | 3-4 |
| 01.6 | **Reading Comprehension — Informational Text** | Text structure (chronological, cause/effect, compare/contrast, problem/solution, description); main idea + supporting details; summarizing vs. retelling; author's purpose (PIE); text features (headings, captions, graphs); nonfiction reading strategies | 4-5 |
| 01.7 | **Writing — Sentences to Paragraphs** | Complete sentences (subject + predicate); sentence types (declarative, interrogative, exclamatory, imperative); capitalization and punctuation; topic sentence + supporting details + concluding sentence; paragraph unity and coherence; narrative writing arc | K-3 (progressive) |
| 01.8 | **Writing — Essays & Research** | 5-paragraph essay structure (intro → 3 body → conclusion); opinion/argumentative writing (claim + reasons + evidence); informative writing; research skills (credible sources, note-taking, paraphrasing vs. quoting); MLA citations intro; revision process | 4-5 |

## 4. App Delivery Notes

- **Audio-first for K-2:** All phonics content needs text-to-speech + read-aloud; many K-2 learners cannot read lesson text independently
- **Decodable readers:** The platform needs a library of decodable texts (matched to phonics scope) — Starfall provides free ones at the early levels
- **Progress markers:** fluency (WCPM rate), phonics scope-and-sequence mastery, writing rubric scores
- **Parent dashboard:** K-2 progress goes to parent/guardian; student-facing UI is purely game-like

---

## 5. Standards Alignment

- **CCSS ELA Standards K-5:** RF (Reading: Foundational Skills), RL (Reading: Literature), RI (Reading: Informational), W (Writing), L (Language)
- **Science of Reading consensus:** aligned to systematic phonics instructional sequence
