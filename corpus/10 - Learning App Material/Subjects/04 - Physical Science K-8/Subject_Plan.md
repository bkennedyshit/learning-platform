*Part of [[Bill's Vault/05-Knowledge_Foundation/10 - Learning App Material/README|10 - Learning App Material]]*

## 3. Chapter Outline

| # | Chapter | Core skill | Grade |
|---|---------|-----------|-------|
| 04.1 | **Forces & Motion — Foundations** | Pushes and pulls; gravity as a force; contact vs. non-contact forces; describing motion (position, speed, direction); balanced vs. unbalanced forces; Newton's first law (intro — objects keep doing what they're doing unless a force acts) | K-2 |
| 04.2 | **Energy — Forms & Transfers** | Forms of energy (kinetic, potential, thermal, light, sound, chemical, electrical); energy transfers and transformations; conservation of energy (energy doesn't disappear, it changes form); simple machines (lever, pulley, wheel-and-axle, inclined plane, wedge, screw) | 3-5 |
| 04.3 | **Waves — Light & Sound** | What is a wave? (disturbance that transfers energy, not matter); sound waves (compression waves, pitch = frequency, volume = amplitude); light waves (transverse, electromagnetic spectrum); reflection, refraction, absorption; how eyes and ears detect waves | 4-6 |
| 04.4 | **Matter — Properties & States** | States of matter (solid, liquid, gas, plasma); physical properties (mass, volume, density, color, hardness, conductivity); changes of state (melting, freezing, evaporation, condensation, sublimation); mixtures vs. pure substances; separation techniques | 4-6 |
| 04.5 | **Forces & Motion — Newton's Laws** | Newton's three laws of motion (formal statement and quantitative intro); net force and acceleration (F=ma qualitative); gravity and weight; friction; free body diagrams; projectile motion intro | 7-8 |
| 04.6 | **Atoms, Elements & the Periodic Table** | Atomic structure (protons, neutrons, electrons); atomic number and mass; the periodic table (periods, groups, metals/nonmetals/metalloids); electron shells and valence electrons; chemical symbols and formulas; the 10 most important elements | 7-8 |
| 04.7 | **Chemical Reactions** | Physical vs. chemical changes; signs of a chemical reaction (color change, gas production, precipitate, temperature change); reactants and products; conservation of mass; balancing equations (by inspection); types of reactions (synthesis, decomposition, single/double replacement, combustion) | 8 |
| 04.8 | **Electricity & Magnetism** | Electric charge (positive/negative); conductors and insulators; circuits (series and parallel); Ohm's Law intro (V=IR qualitative); magnets and magnetic fields; electromagnetism (current → magnetic field); electric motors and generators intro | 7-8 |
