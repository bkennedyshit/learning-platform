## 📚 Authoritative Catalogs & Sources

_Pulled verbatim from `Subject_Plan.md §2 — Free Learning Catalog`. Source of truth is the Subject_Plan; this section is a convenience copy._

- **[OpenStax Algebra & Trig 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/algebra-and-trigonometry-2e)** — free, rigorous, covers chapters 16.1-16.8 in full
- **[OpenStax Precalculus 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/precalculus-2e)** — free; specifically the Precalculus volume
- **[Khan Academy Algebra II](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra2)** — complete free practice
- **[Khan Academy Precalculus](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/precalculus)** — trig + conic sections + series
- **[Professor Leonard — Precalculus (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@ProfessorLeonard)** — full semester course; exceptionally clear
- **[Paul's Online Math Notes — Algebra](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/Alg/Alg.aspx)** — free exhaustive reference
- **[Desmos](https://www.desmos.com/calculator)** — graph every function in this course; non-optional
- **[3Blue1Brown — Essence of Trigonometry (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@3blue1brown)** — visual intuition for trig
