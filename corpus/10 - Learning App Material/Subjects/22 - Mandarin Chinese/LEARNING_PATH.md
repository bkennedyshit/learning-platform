## ⚠️ Non-Negotiable Prerequisites

Before starting or alongside starting this track:

1. **Install Pleco (free)** — the essential Mandarin dictionary app. Every unknown character → Pleco. Never study without it open.
2. **Set up Anki HSK decks** — import HSK 1 deck immediately. Add 10 new cards/day maximum; review every single day.
3. **Accept the tones** — four tones are not optional. Tones *are* the language. Budget 20 min/day purely on tone production for the first 4 weeks.
4. **Understand the timeline** — HSK 4 from zero takes ~12 months at 1 hr/day. This is not discouraging — it is accurate. Mandarin rewards patience.

## 📖 Recommended Chapter Order

### Phase 1: Foundation — Tones, Pinyin & First Characters (Weeks 1–6)

| Order | Chapter | Time | Deliverable |
|-------|---------|------|------------|
| 1 | [[22.1 - Tones, Pinyin & The Sound System]] | 2 weeks | Produce all 4 tones correctly; read any pinyin syllable; know tone sandhi rules |
| 2 | [[22.2 - Characters — Radicals & The Writing System]] | 2 weeks (ongoing) | Know 50 basic radicals; write 100 HSK 1 characters with correct stroke order |
| 3 | [[22.3 - Basic Sentences & Grammar I — HSK 1-2]] | 2 weeks | Introduce yourself; ask basic questions; count; use measure words for 个/本/张 |

**Phase 1 deliverable:** Pass simulated HSK 1 practice exam. Produce all tones without coaching.

### Phase 2: Grammar Core + Character Building (Weeks 7–16)

| Order | Chapter | Time | Deliverable |
|-------|---------|------|------------|
| 4 | [[22.4 - Aspect Markers & Time — Grammar II HSK 2-3]] | 3 weeks | Use 了/过/着 correctly; express time; narrate a simple past event |
| 5 | [[22.6 - The Character System in Depth]] | Ongoing (run parallel) | Recognize 600 HSK 1-3 characters; read simple texts |

**Phase 2 deliverable:** Pass simulated HSK 2. Read a 100-character HSK-2 text at 70%+ comprehension.

### Phase 3: Complex Grammar + Intermediate Literacy (Weeks 17–36)

| Order | Chapter | Time | Deliverable |
|-------|---------|------|------------|
| 6 | [[22.5 - Serial Verbs, 把/被 & Resultative Complements]] | 4 weeks | Use 把 construction; passive 被; resultative complements |
| Continue 22.6 | Character depth | Ongoing | HSK 4 character recognition (1,200 characters) |

**Phase 3 deliverable:** Pass simulated HSK 3. Have a 5-minute conversation on a familiar topic.

### Phase 4: Fluency & Cultural Integration (Weeks 37–52)

| Order | Chapter | Deliverable |
|-------|---------|------------|
| 7 | [[22.7 - Idioms, Chengyu & Cultural Register]] | Know 20 chéngyǔ; navigate formal vs. informal register |
| 8 | [[22.8 - Reading, Listening & Cultural Immersion]] | Pass HSK 4. Read DuChinese intermediate texts at 80%+ comprehension |

## 🔀 Non-Negotiable Daily Habits

| Habit | Time | Phase |
|-------|------|-------|
| Anki HSK deck review (characters + tone + meaning) | 15–20 min | Every phase |
| Tone production (minimal pairs aloud) | 5 min | Phase 1-2 |
| Pleco character lookup — every unknown | As needed | Every phase |
| Listening (Mandarin Corner / ChinesePod) | 15–20 min | Phase 2+ |
| Pleco stroke order writing practice | 10 min | Phase 1-3 |

> ⚠️ **Even a 3-day break in the first 6 months causes measurable regression in tones and characters.** Daily practice is not a suggestion — it is the method.

## ➡️ What Comes Next After This Track

1. **HSK 5** — vocabulary doubles to 2,500; formal written Chinese; authentic newspapers
2. **Classical Chinese (文言文 wényánwén)** — the literary language of pre-20th-century texts; relates to Chinese as Latin relates to Romance languages
3. **Business Mandarin** — specialized vocabulary for commerce, negotiation, formal presentations
4. **[Mandarin Blueprint](https://www.mandarinblueprint.com/)** — advanced character-learning system; recommended for post-HSK 4
