## 📚 Authoritative Catalogs & Sources

_Pulled verbatim from `Subject_Plan.md §2 — Free Learning Catalog`. Source of truth is the Subject_Plan; this section is a convenience copy._

### Phonetics & Speaking
- **[ChinesePod (YouTube free tier)](https://www.youtube.com/@ChinesePod)** — structured dialogues from absolute beginner through upper-intermediate; the standard Mandarin podcast learning resource
- **[Yoyo Chinese (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@yoyochinese)** — Yangyang Cheng's systematic grammar series; excellent for English speakers; free comprehensive content
- **[Mandarin Corner (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@MandarinCorner)** — comprehensible input; slow speech; topics for beginners through advanced
- **[Chinese Zero to Hero (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@ChineseZeroToHero)** — HSK-aligned course; clear, structured
- **[HSK Academy (free)](https://www.hskacademy.com/)** — free HSK 1-6 vocabulary lists, exercises, and tests

### Character Study
- **[Remembering the Hanzi](https://www.amazon.com/Remembering-Simplified-Hanzi-Meaning-Characters/dp/0824833236)** — James Heisig's mnemonics-based character memory system (book; library)
- **[Skritter (limited free)](https://skritter.com/)** — best character stroke-order practice app; some free content
- **[Pleco (iOS/Android — free)](https://www.pleco.com/)** — the best Mandarin dictionary app; handwriting input; stroke order animations; essential tool ← **install immediately**
- **[MDBG Chinese Dictionary](https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary)** — free web dictionary with pinyin, characters, and stroke order
- **[Outlier Linguistics Dictionary](https://www.outlier-linguistics.com/)** — etymology-based character learning; explains why characters look the way they do

### Textbooks
- **[HSK Standard Course (1-6)](https://www.blcup.com/)** — the official HSK exam textbook series; free PDFs available via library
- **[New Practical Chinese Reader](https://www.sinolingua.com.cn/)** — the standard university Mandarin textbook used in China; 6 volumes
- **[Integrated Chinese (3rd ed.)](https://cheng-tsui.com/browse/titles/integrated-chinese)** — the most-used Mandarin textbook in US universities (library)

### Listening & Immersion
- **[Mandarin Corner — Slow Chinese](https://www.youtube.com/@MandarinCorner)** — comprehensible input; essential from chapter 22.3+
- **[HSK Listening Practice (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/)** — search "HSK listening practice" for official exam audio
- **[CCTV News (Chinese)](https://www.youtube.com/@CCTVNews)** — native Chinese news; B2+ level
- **[Bilibili](https://www.bilibili.com/)** — Chinese YouTube; vast native content; no account needed for browsing

### Spaced Repetition
- **[Anki — HSK Vocabulary decks](https://ankiweb.net/shared/decks/hsk)** — community HSK decks with audio; essential from day one ← **non-negotiable**
- **[DuChinese](https://www.duchinese.net/)** — graded Chinese readers with tap-to-translate; excellent for chapter literacy stages
