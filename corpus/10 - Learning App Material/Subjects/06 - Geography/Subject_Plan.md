## 3. Chapter Outline

| # | Chapter | Core skill |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 06.1 | **Map Skills & Spatial Thinking** | Types of maps (physical, political, thematic, topographic, climate); map elements (title, legend, compass rose, scale, grid); latitude and longitude; time zones; map projections (Mercator, Peters, Robinson) and their distortions; reading and interpreting data maps |
| 06.2 | **Physical Geography — Landforms & Bodies of Water** | Major landforms (continents, mountain ranges, plains, plateaus, valleys, deltas); major bodies of water (oceans, seas, rivers, lakes, bays, straits); how landforms are created (tectonic, erosional, depositional); topographic maps |
| 06.3 | **Climate & Biomes** | Climate vs. weather; climate factors (latitude, altitude, proximity to water, ocean currents, prevailing winds); Köppen climate classification; world biomes and their characteristics; climate change impacts on biomes |
| 06.4 | **Human Geography — Population & Migration** | World population distribution; factors affecting population density; demographic transition model; push and pull factors of migration; major migration patterns (historical and contemporary); urbanization; megacities |
| 06.5 | **Cultural Geography** | Culture components (language, religion, customs, government, economy); cultural diffusion; cultural landscapes; major world religions and their geographic distribution; language families; culture regions |
| 06.6 | **Political Geography** | States, nations, nation-states; types of government; political boundaries (natural vs. artificial); geopolitics; international organizations (UN, NATO, EU, ASEAN); conflict and cooperation; colonialism's geographic legacy |
| 06.7 | **Economic Geography** | Economic systems (traditional, command, market, mixed); development indicators (GDP per capita, HDI, literacy, infant mortality); world trade patterns; resource distribution; economic globalization; North-South divide |
| 06.8 | **Environmental Geography & Sustainability** | Human impact on the environment; deforestation, desertification, water scarcity; climate change geography; renewable vs. nonrenewable resources by region; sustainable development; environmental justice |
