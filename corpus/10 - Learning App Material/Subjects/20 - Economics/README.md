## 📚 Authoritative Catalogs & Sources

_Pulled verbatim from `Subject_Plan.md §2 — Free Learning Catalog`. Source of truth is the Subject_Plan; this section is a convenience copy._

### Microeconomics
- **[OpenStax Principles of Microeconomics 3e](https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-microeconomics-3e)** — free, peer-reviewed; covers chapters 20.1-20.5 ← **primary micro text**
- **[Khan Academy AP Microeconomics](https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/ap-microeconomics)** — complete AP Micro; excellent graph practice
- **[Jacob Clifford / ACDCLeadership (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@ACDCLeadership)** — the AP Economics channel; clear, concise, graph-focused

### Macroeconomics
- **[OpenStax Principles of Macroeconomics 3e](https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-macroeconomics-3e)** — free; covers chapters 20.6-20.8 ← **primary macro text**
- **[Khan Academy AP Macroeconomics](https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/ap-macroeconomics)** — complete AP Macro
- **[Crash Course Economics (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@crashcourse)** — 35-episode economics series

### Reference
- **[EconLib (Library of Economics & Liberty)](https://www.econlib.org/)** — free encyclopedia of economics concepts
- **[Federal Reserve Education](https://www.federalreserveeducation.org/)** — free Fed resources; monetary policy explained by the source
- **[FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data)](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/)** — free real economic data; use for every macro chapter
