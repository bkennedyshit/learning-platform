# 🏫 6-8 Middle School Pipeline

> Full subject design for grades 6 through 8 (ages 11–14). This is the most structurally important grade band — it's where learners transition from learning to read to reading to learn, and where math branches into formal algebra.

*Back to [[K-12 Curriculum Pipeline]] · [[Bill's Vault/README]]*

---

## Pedagogical Philosophy for 6-8

Middle school learners are in a critical neurological transition:
- **Abstract reasoning emerges** — formal operational thinking begins around age 11-12 (Piaget). Algebra becomes possible. Historical causality becomes understandable.
- **Social motivation peaks** — peer learning, collaboration, and discussion-based learning are highly effective. Isolation-based instruction is least effective here.
- **Metacognition develops** — students can start to think about their own thinking. Study skills, spaced repetition, and retrieval practice should be explicitly taught.
- **Interest-based hooks** — abstract topics land better when anchored to things that matter to this age group: fairness, identity, technology, sports, music.

## Mathematics Track (Grades 6-8)

**Vault location:** `09 - Learning/13 - Pre-Algebra/`
**Standards:** CCSS 6.RP, 6.NS, 6.EE, 6.G, 7.RP, 7.NS, 7.EE

### 46 — Geometry (Grade 9-10, or Grade 8 for accelerated)

**Vault location:** `09 - Learning/15 - Geometry/`
**Standards:** CCSS HSG.CO, HSG.SRT, HSG.C, HSG.GPE, HSG.GMD

| Chapter | Core skill |
|---------|-----------|
| 46.1 | Foundations of Geometry — undefined terms (point, line, plane); postulates; segments, rays, angles |
| 46.2 | Reasoning & Proof — inductive vs. deductive reasoning; two-column proofs; logical connectives |
| 46.3 | Parallel & Perpendicular Lines — transversal angle pairs; proving lines parallel; slope connections |
| 46.4 | Triangles — triangle sum; exterior angle theorem; congruence (SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, HL) |
| 46.5 | Similarity & Proportions — similar figures; AA, SAS, SSS similarity; scale factors; dilations |
| 46.6 | Right Triangles & Trigonometry — Pythagorean theorem; special right triangles (30-60-90, 45-45-90); SOH-CAH-TOA |
| 46.7 | Circles — arcs, chords, inscribed angles, tangent lines; circle equations |
| 46.8 | Area, Volume & Coordinate Geometry — 2D area formulas; 3D volume/surface area; coordinate proofs |

**Prerequisite:** Track 14 — Algebra I (slope, equations, coordinate plane)
**Unlocks:** Track 16 — Algebra II/Pre-Calc
