## Math Track (Grades 9-12)

### 47 — Algebra II & Pre-Calculus (Grade 10-11)

**Vault:** `09 - Learning/16 - Algebra II & Pre-Calculus/`
**Standards:** CCSS HSA, HSF, HSN, HSG

| Chapter | Core skill |
|---------|-----------|
| 47.1 | Polynomial Functions — synthetic division, remainder/factor theorem, complex roots, end behavior |
| 47.2 | Rational Functions — asymptotes (vertical, horizontal, oblique), holes, graphing, partial fractions |
| 47.3 | Exponential & Logarithmic Functions — properties of logs, change of base, exponential growth/decay, natural log |
| 47.4 | Trigonometry — unit circle, radian measure, all 6 trig functions, reference angles, exact values |
| 47.5 | Trig Identities & Equations — Pythagorean identities, sum/difference formulas, double-angle, solving trig equations |
| 47.6 | Conic Sections — parabola, ellipse, hyperbola, circle — standard form, graphing, applications |
| 47.7 | Sequences, Series & Binomial Theorem — arithmetic/geometric sequences, sigma notation, binomial theorem |
| 47.8 | Limits & Introduction to Calculus — intuitive limits, continuity, average vs. instantaneous rate of change |

**Prerequisite:** Track 15 — Geometry, Track 14 — Algebra I
**Unlocks:** Track 01 — Calculus (Math & Physics tower)

**Free resources:**
- Khan Academy Algebra II — [khanacademy.org/math/algebra2](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra2)
- Khan Academy Precalculus — [khanacademy.org/math/precalculus](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/precalculus)
- OpenStax Algebra & Trigonometry 2e — [openstax.org](https://openstax.org/details/books/algebra-and-trigonometry-2e)
- Desmos — [desmos.com](https://www.desmos.com/) (graphing all conic sections and trig functions)
- Paul's Online Math Notes — [tutorial.math.lamar.edu](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/) (free, exhaustive)

### 48 — Statistics & Probability (Grade 11-12 / AP Stats)

**Vault:** `09 - Learning/17 - Statistics & Probability/`
**Standards:** CCSS HSS; College Board AP Statistics

| Chapter | Core skill |
|---------|-----------|
| 48.1 | Exploring Data (1-Variable) — distributions, shape/center/spread, boxplots, histograms, outliers |
| 48.2 | Exploring Data (2-Variable) — scatterplots, correlation, least-squares regression, residuals |
| 48.3 | Sampling & Experimental Design — random sampling methods, bias, observational vs. experimental, control/treatment |
| 48.4 | Probability — sample space, addition/multiplication rules, conditional probability, independence, Bayes |
| 48.5 | Discrete Random Variables & Distributions — expected value, variance, binomial distribution, geometric distribution |
| 48.6 | Normal Distribution & Sampling Distributions — z-scores, normal curve, Central Limit Theorem, sampling dist. of x̄ and p̂ |
| 48.7 | Confidence Intervals — 1-sample z and t intervals for mean and proportion; interpretation; margin of error |
| 48.8 | Significance Tests (Hypothesis Testing) — 1 and 2 sample z/t tests, chi-square, ANOVA intro; Type I/II error |

**Prerequisite:** Track 13/45 — Pre-Algebra / Algebra I (basic algebraic fluency)
**Unlocks:** Track 23 — AI/ML (statistics is the foundation of all ML), data science work

**AP alignment:** This track covers the full AP Statistics curriculum (College Board CED 2024).

**Free resources:**
- OpenStax Introductory Statistics 2e — [openstax.org](https://openstax.org/details/books/introductory-statistics-2e) ← **primary text**
- AP Stats Guy (YouTube) — [youtube.com/@APStatisticsGuy](https://www.youtube.com/@APStatisticsGuy)
- Stats with Professor B (YouTube) — [youtube.com/@ProfessorBStats](https://www.youtube.com/@ProfessorBStats)
- Khan Academy AP Statistics — [khanacademy.org/math/ap-statistics](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/ap-statistics)
- StatQuest with Josh Starmer (YouTube) — [youtube.com/@statquest](https://www.youtube.com/@statquest) (excellent for intuition)

## History / Social Studies Track (Grades 9-12)

### 49 — World History (Grade 9-10 / AP World History)

**Vault:** `09 - Learning/18 - World History/`
**AP alignment:** AP World History: Modern (College Board, 1200 CE → present)

| Chapter | Core skill |
|---------|-----------|
| 49.1 | The Global Tapestry (1200-1450) — Song China, Dar al-Islam, Mali Empire, Byzantine; trade networks |
| 49.2 | Networks of Exchange (1200-1450) — Mongol Empire, Silk Roads, Indian Ocean Trade, Black Death |
| 49.3 | Land-Based Empires (1450-1750) — Ottoman, Safavid, Mughal, Qing, Russian; gunpowder empires |
| 49.4 | Transoceanic Interconnections (1450-1750) — European maritime empires, Columbian Exchange, Atlantic slave trade |
| 49.5 | Revolutions (1750-1900) — Enlightenment ideas, American/French/Haitian Revolutions, nationalism, independence movements |
| 49.6 | Consequences of Industrialization (1750-1900) — Industrial Revolution, imperialism, migration, environmental change |
| 49.7 | Global Conflict & Its Consequences (1900-present) — WWI, WWII, Cold War, decolonization, genocide |
| 49.8 | Globalization (1900-present) — post-WWII economy, UN/institutions, technology, inequality, climate |

### 50 — US History (Grade 11 / AP US History)

**Vault:** `09 - Learning/19 - US History/`
**AP alignment:** AP United States History (APUSH) — College Board

| Chapter | Core skill |
|---------|-----------|
| 50.1 | Colonial America (1491-1754) — Native American societies; Spanish, French, British colonization; colonial societies; slavery's origins |
| 50.2 | Revolution & Early Republic (1754-1800) — Seven Years' War, Revolution, Articles of Confederation, Constitution, Federalist era |
| 50.3 | Expansion & Reform (1800-1848) — Jeffersonian democracy, War of 1812, Market Revolution, reform movements, Manifest Destiny |
| 50.4 | Civil War Era (1844-1877) — causes of Civil War, war itself, Reconstruction — its achievements and dismantling |
| 50.5 | Gilded Age & Progressivism (1877-1920) — industrialization, immigration, urbanization, Progressive Era reforms, imperialism |
| 50.6 | Depression & World Wars (1920-1945) — 1920s culture, Great Depression, New Deal, WWII at home and abroad |
| 50.7 | Cold War America (1945-1980) — containment, Korean War, Civil Rights Movement, Vietnam, Great Society, Nixon, social upheaval |
| 50.8 | Contemporary America (1980-present) — Reagan revolution, end of Cold War, Clinton era, 9/11, Obama, polarization, 21st century |

### 51 — Economics (Grade 12 / AP Micro + AP Macro)

**Vault:** `09 - Learning/20 - Economics/`
**AP alignment:** AP Microeconomics + AP Macroeconomics (College Board) — covers both

| Chapter | Core skill |
|---------|-----------|
| 51.1 | Economic Thinking & Scarcity — opportunity cost, marginal analysis, PPF (production possibilities frontier), comparative advantage |
| 51.2 | Supply & Demand — law of demand/supply, elasticity (price, income, cross), market equilibrium, price controls (floors/ceilings) |
| 51.3 | Market Structures — perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly, monopolistic competition; profit maximization (MR=MC) |
| 51.4 | Factor Markets & Distribution of Income — labor market, wage determination, capital, land; income inequality |
| 51.5 | Market Failures & Government — externalities, public goods, information asymmetry, government intervention |
| 51.6 | Macroeconomic Indicators — GDP (components), unemployment (types), inflation (CPI, causes), business cycle |
| 51.7 | Aggregate Demand & Supply — AD-AS model, short-run vs. long-run equilibrium, fiscal policy (multiplier effect) |
| 51.8 | Money, Banking & Monetary Policy — money supply (M1/M2), Federal Reserve, interest rates, monetary transmission, Phillips Curve |
