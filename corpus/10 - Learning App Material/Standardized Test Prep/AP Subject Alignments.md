# 🎯 AP Subject Alignments

> Maps every major AP exam to the specific vault tracks and chapters that cover it. Use this to build AP-mode filters on the platform.

*Cross-reference: [[Bill's Vault/05-Knowledge_Foundation/10 - Learning App Material/README]] · [[Grade Band Pipelines/9-12 High School Pipeline]]*

## How to Read This Document

Each AP exam entry shows:
- **Vault tracks** that cover the material
- **Coverage level** (Full / Partial / Stub)
- **Key gaps** — what's not yet covered

The platform's "AP Mode" toggle will filter chapter content to exactly what appears on each AP exam and add FRQ (free-response question) formatting to drill scripts.

## AP Sciences

### AP Biology
Track 02 — Biology covers all 8 AP Bio units (biochemistry, cell processes, cellular energetics, heredity, natural selection, ecology).
**Overall: ✅ Full coverage**

### AP Chemistry
Track 03 — Chemistry covers all 9 AP Chem units.
**Overall: ✅ Full coverage**

### AP Physics 1 & 2
Track 01 — Physics chapters cover all AP Physics 1 (mechanics, waves) and AP Physics 2 (thermodynamics, E&M, optics) content.
**Overall: ✅ Full coverage**

### AP Physics C: Mechanics
Track 01 — Mechanics + Calculus integration. Requires AP Calculus BC prerequisite.
**Overall: ✅ Full coverage**

### AP Environmental Science
Track 21 — Earth Science covers Units 1-3 (Earth systems, biodiversity, population) and Unit 8 (aquatic/terrestrial pollution).
**Gap:** Units 4-7 (land use, energy, atmospheric pollution, global change) — partially covered in 52.8.
**Overall: 🟡 ~65% coverage. Track 67 (planned) will complete this.**

### AP Anatomy & Physiology (where offered)
Track 40 — Anatomy covers all 8 body systems at clinical depth.
**Overall: ✅ Full coverage**
