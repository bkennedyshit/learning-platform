### Problem 1 — 2×2 determinant

Compute $\det A$ for $A = \left[\begin{matrix}0 & 2\\1 & -4\end{matrix}\right]$ using the formula $\det = ad - bc$. State whether $A$ is invertible and give the geometric meaning.

<details>

<summary>Show solution</summary>

$\det A = (0)(-4) - (2)(1) = 0 - 2 = -2$.

Since $\det A = -2 \neq 0$, $A$ is **invertible**.

Geometrically: $A$ scales areas by $|-2|$ and reverses orientation (det < 0).

</details>

### Problem 2 — 2×2 determinant

Compute $\det A$ for $A = \left[\begin{matrix}-4 & -2\\2 & -1\end{matrix}\right]$ using the formula $\det = ad - bc$. State whether $A$ is invertible and give the geometric meaning.

<details>

<summary>Show solution</summary>

$\det A = (-4)(-1) - (-2)(2) = 4 - -4 = 8$.

Since $\det A = 8 \neq 0$, $A$ is **invertible**.

Geometrically: $A$ scales areas by $|8|$ and preserves orientation (det > 0).

</details>

### Problem 3 — det(AB) = det(A)det(B) verification

Let $A = \left[\begin{matrix}-2 & 3\\-2 & 3\end{matrix}\right]$ and $B = \left[\begin{matrix}2 & 1\\0 & -3\end{matrix}\right]$. Compute $\det A$, $\det B$, $\det(AB)$, and verify $\det(AB) = \det(A)\det(B)$.

<details>

<summary>Show solution</summary>

$\det A = 0$, $\det B = -6$.

$$
AB = \left[\begin{matrix}-4 & -11\\-4 & -11\end{matrix}\right], \quad \det(AB) = 0.
$$

Check: $\det(A)\det(B) = 0 \cdot -6 = 0 = \det(AB)$. ✓

</details>

### Problem 4 — Cramer's Rule (2×2 system)

Solve $A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$ via **Cramer's Rule** where $A = \left[\begin{matrix}4 & -1\\2 & -4\end{matrix}\right]$ and $\mathbf{b} = \left[\begin{matrix}4\\-5\end{matrix}\right]$.

<details>

<summary>Show solution</summary>

$\det(A) = -14$.

Replace column 1 with $\mathbf{b}$: $A_1 = \left[\begin{matrix}4 & -1\\-5 & -4\end{matrix}\right]$, $\det(A_1) = -21$.

Replace column 2 with $\mathbf{b}$: $A_2 = \left[\begin{matrix}4 & 4\\2 & -5\end{matrix}\right]$, $\det(A_2) = -28$.

$$
x_1 = \frac{-21}{-14} = \frac{3}{2}, \quad x_2 = \frac{-28}{-14} = 2.
$$

Verify: $A\left[\begin{matrix}\frac{3}{2}\\2\end{matrix}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}4\\-5\end{matrix}\right]$. ✓

</details>
