## 3. Chapter Outline

| # | Chapter | Core skill | Grade |
|---|---------|-----------|-------|
| 03.1 | **Living vs. Non-Living & Basic Needs** | Characteristics of living things (MRS GREN: movement, respiration, sensitivity, growth, reproduction, excretion, nutrition); basic needs of plants and animals; habitats; structures and functions in organisms | K-1 |
| 03.2 | **Plants — Structure, Function & Life Cycles** | Plant parts and their functions (roots, stems, leaves, flowers, seeds); photosynthesis intro; plant life cycles; seed dispersal strategies; adaptations of plants to different environments | 2-3 |
| 03.3 | **Animals — Behavior, Adaptations & Life Cycles** | Animal life cycles (incomplete vs. complete metamorphosis); inherited vs. learned behaviors; physical and behavioral adaptations; survival strategies; comparative anatomy of vertebrates vs. invertebrates | 3-4 |
| 03.4 | **Ecosystems & Food Webs** | Ecosystem components (biotic/abiotic); producers, consumers (herbivore/carnivore/omnivore), decomposers; food chains and food webs; energy flow (10% rule intro); ecosystem services; biomes overview (forest, desert, grassland, tundra, aquatic) | 4-5 |
| 03.5 | **Cells — The Basic Unit of Life** | Cell theory; prokaryotes vs. eukaryotes; plant vs. animal cells; organelles and their functions (nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplast, cell membrane, cell wall, vacuole, ribosomes); cell processes (respiration, photosynthesis as cellular processes) | 6-7 |
| 03.6 | **Genetics & Heredity** | DNA as the instruction manual; genes and chromosomes; dominant and recessive traits; Punnett squares (monohybrid cross); genotype vs. phenotype; incomplete dominance; sex-linked traits intro; variation within populations | 7-8 |
| 03.7 | **Evolution & Natural Selection** | Darwin's voyage and observations; natural selection (variation → heritability → differential survival/reproduction → change over time); evidence for evolution (fossil record, comparative anatomy, DNA); speciation; common ancestry; adaptation as outcome of natural selection | 7-8 |
| 03.8 | **Human Body Systems** | Overview of 11 body systems and how they interact; focus on digestive, circulatory, respiratory, and nervous systems; homeostasis; disease and immune response; the connection to Track 40 Anatomy for full depth | 7-8 |

## 5. Standards Alignment
- **NGSS:** LS1 (From Molecules to Organisms), LS2 (Ecosystems), LS3 (Heredity), LS4 (Biological Evolution)
- **Progression:** K-2 (observations of organisms) → 3-5 (patterns and functions) → 6-8 (mechanisms and systems)
- **Connects to:** Track 02 Biology (full AP depth), Track 40 Anatomy (body systems)
