## 📖 Recommended Chapter Order

Follow chapters 14.1 → 14.8 in sequence. Each chapter builds on the previous. Do not skip chapters.

| Phase | Chapters | Deliverable |
|-------|---------|------------|
| **Phase 1** | 14.1 – 14.3 | Core conceptual foundations; pass a diagnostic test on the first three topics |
| **Phase 2** | 14.4 – 14.6 | Application and problem-solving; attempt at least 20 practice problems per chapter |
| **Phase 3** | 14.7 – 14.8 | Synthesis and extension; complete a full-length practice exam before considering the track complete |

---

## 🔀 Daily Study Habits

| Habit | Time | Why |
|-------|------|-----|
| Active problem-solving (not passive re-reading) | 20–30 min | Retrieval practice is the highest-leverage activity |
| Review errors from previous session | 5–10 min | Errors are the most efficient learning signal |
| Khan Academy / drill script practice | 10–15 min | Interleaved practice beats blocked practice |
