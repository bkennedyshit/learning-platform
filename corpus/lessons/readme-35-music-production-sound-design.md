## 🔭 2026 Industry Snapshot

| Area | 2026 Reality |
|------|-------------|
| AI music | MusicGen (Meta, open), Stable Audio Open (Stability, Apache), Suno/Udio (commercial) |
| Spatial audio | Apple Atmos for Music, Dolby Atmos — becoming distribution standard |
| Stems separation | Demucs 4 (Meta, open-source) at near-pro quality |
| Plugin format | VST3 + AU + CLAP (new open format gaining adoption) |
| DAW landscape | Ableton Live, Logic (Mac), FL Studio dominate; LMMS is best free cross-platform option |
