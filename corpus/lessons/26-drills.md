### Problem 1 — Characteristic polynomial

Compute the characteristic polynomial $p(\lambda) = \det(A - \lambda I)$ for $A = \left[\begin{matrix}2 & -3\\-3 & 2\end{matrix}\right]$.

<details>

<summary>Show solution</summary>

$$
A - \lambda I = \left[\begin{matrix}2 - \lambda & -3\\-3 & 2 - \lambda\end{matrix}\right].
$$

$$
p(\lambda) = (2-\lambda)(2-\lambda) - (-3)(-3).
$$

Expand: $\lambda^2 - 4\lambda + (-5) = \lambda^{2} - 4 \lambda - 5$.

**Trace check:** coefficient of $-\lambda$ should be $\operatorname{tr}(A) = 4$. ✓

**Det check:** constant term should be $\det(A) = -5$. ✓

</details>

### Problem 2 — Finding eigenvalues

Find all eigenvalues of $A = \left[\begin{matrix}2 & \frac{5}{2} & \frac{5}{2}\\1 & - \frac{1}{2} & - \frac{3}{2}\\-1 & - \frac{5}{2} & - \frac{3}{2}\end{matrix}\right]$ by solving $\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$.

<details>

<summary>Show solution</summary>

Characteristic polynomial: $p(\lambda) = \lambda^{3} - 7 \lambda + 6$.

Solving $p(\lambda) = 0$: eigenvalues are $\lambda = -3, \lambda = 1, \lambda = 2$.

Verification: $\operatorname{tr}(A) = 0 = -3 + 1 + 2$. ✓  $\det(A) = -6 = -3 \cdot 1 \cdot 2$. ✓

</details>

### Problem 3 — Finding eigenvectors

For $A = \left[\begin{matrix}-2 & 0\\1 & -1\end{matrix}\right]$, find eigenvectors for each eigenvalue.

<details>

<summary>Show solution</summary>

**$\lambda = -2$:** Solve $(A - -2I)\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}$:

$$
\left[\begin{matrix}0 & 0\\1 & 1\end{matrix}\right]\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}.
$$

Eigenvector: $\mathbf{v} = \left[\begin{matrix}-1\\1\end{matrix}\right]$ (up to scalar). Check: $A\left[\begin{matrix}-1\\1\end{matrix}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}2\\-2\end{matrix}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}2\\-2\end{matrix}\right]$. ✓

**$\lambda = -1$:** Solve $(A - -1I)\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}$:

$$
\left[\begin{matrix}-1 & 0\\1 & 0\end{matrix}\right]\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0}.
$$

Eigenvector: $\mathbf{v} = \left[\begin{matrix}0\\1\end{matrix}\right]$ (up to scalar). Check: $A\left[\begin{matrix}0\\1\end{matrix}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}0\\-1\end{matrix}\right] = \left[\begin{matrix}0\\-1\end{matrix}\right]$. ✓

</details>
