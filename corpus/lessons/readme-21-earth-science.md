## 📚 Authoritative Catalogs & Sources

_Pulled verbatim from `Subject_Plan.md §2 — Free Learning Catalog`. Source of truth is the Subject_Plan; this section is a convenience copy._

- **[CK-12 Earth Science for Middle School](https://www.ck12.org/book/ck-12-earth-science-for-middle-school/)** — free, adaptive; the primary text for this track ← **start here**
- **[Crash Course Astronomy (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@crashcourse)** — 46-episode series covers the solar system and universe
- **[Crash Course Geography (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@crashcourse)** — Earth systems framing
- **[USGS Education Portal](https://www.usgs.gov/education)** — authoritative free resources; plate tectonics, earthquakes, volcanoes
- **[NASA Earth Observatory](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/)** — free satellite imagery, climate science articles, and data
- **[NOAA Education Resources](https://www.noaa.gov/education)** — free oceanography, weather, and climate materials
- **[Geology Kitchen (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@GeologyKitchen)** — excellent visual geology explanations
- **[SERC Earth Exploration Toolbook](https://serc.carleton.edu/eet/index.html)** — free data analysis activities using real Earth science datasets
- **[PhET Simulations — Earth Science](https://phet.colorado.edu/)** — free interactive simulations for plate tectonics and planetary motion
