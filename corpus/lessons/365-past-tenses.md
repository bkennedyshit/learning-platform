# 36.5 — Past Tenses

> *"The preterite is a snapshot. The imperfect is a film still."*

Spanish has two simple past tenses where English has one: the **preterite** (pretérito indefinido) and the **imperfect** (pretérito imperfecto). English speakers collapse both into "was" and "used to" and "I did" without thinking about it — but Spanish forces you to make the aspectual distinction explicit every time. Master this decision and you can tell stories. Miss it and your past-tense speech will always sound off.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

1. Conjugate regular and major irregular verbs in the preterite.
2. Conjugate regular verbs in the imperfect and memorize the three irregular imperfects.
3. Apply the preterite vs. imperfect decision tree to any past-tense situation.
4. Form the compound past (*he hablado*) and explain when it differs from the preterite.
5. Form the pluperfect (*había hablado*) and use it for "had done" constructions.

## 📚 1. The Preterite (Pretérito Indefinido)

The preterite marks a **completed, bounded action** in the past — something that happened and is done, with a clear start and/or end point.

### Regular endings

| Person | -AR (hablar) | -ER/-IR (comer / vivir) |
|--------|------------|----------------------|
| yo | habl**é** | com**í** / viv**í** |
| tú | habl**aste** | com**iste** / viv**iste** |
| él/ella | habl**ó** | com**ió** / viv**ió** |
| nosotros | habl**amos** | com**imos** / viv**imos** |
| vosotros | habl**asteis** | com**isteis** / viv**isteis** |
| ellos | habl**aron** | com**ieron** / viv**ieron** |

> **Note:** *nosotros* form is **identical** to present for -AR and -IR verbs — context determines tense.

### Major irregular preterites

These are memorized as complete paradigms — they do not follow the regular endings:

| Verb | Stem | Yo | Tú | Él | Nos | Ellos |
|------|------|----|----|----|----|-------|
| *ser / ir* | fue- | fui | fuiste | fue | fuimos | fueron |
| *tener* | tuv- | tuve | tuviste | tuvo | tuvimos | tuvieron |
| *estar* | estuv- | estuve | estuviste | estuvo | estuvimos | estuvieron |
| *hacer* | hic-/hiz- | hice | hiciste | hizo | hicimos | hicieron |
| *poder* | pud- | pude | pudiste | pudo | pudimos | pudieron |
| *poner* | pus- | puse | pusiste | puso | pusimos | pusieron |
| *saber* | sup- | supe | supiste | supo | supimos | supieron |
| *querer* | quis- | quise | quisiste | quiso | quisimos | quisieron |
| *venir* | vin- | vine | viniste | vino | vinimos | vinieron |
| *decir* | dij- | dije | dijiste | dijo | dijimos | dijeron |
| *traer* | traj- | traje | trajiste | trajo | trajimos | trajeron |

> **Pattern:** Most of these irregular preterite stems share the same endings: *-e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron* — NO accent marks on yo and él forms (unlike regular preterites).

### Spelling-change preterites (-CAR, -GAR, -ZAR verbs — yo form only)

| Type | Yo change | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| -CAR | c → qu | *buscar → busqué* |
| -GAR | g → gu | *llegar → llegué* |
| -ZAR | z → c | *empezar → empecé* |

Only the *yo* form changes — all other forms are regular.

### Stem-changing verbs in preterite (-IR only)

-IR verbs with stem changes also change in the **él/ella** and **ellos/ellas** preterite forms:
- *dormir* (o→ue): *durmió, durmieron*
- *servir* (e→i): *sirvió, sirvieron*
- *pedir* (e→i): *pidió, pidieron*
- *preferir* (e→ie): *prefirió, prefirieron*
- *morir* (o→ue): *murió, murieron*

## 📚 2. The Imperfect (Pretérito Imperfecto)

The imperfect marks **ongoing, habitual, or incomplete** past states and actions — no specified beginning or end.

### Regular endings (only 3 irregulars in the entire language!)

| Person | -AR (hablar) | -ER/-IR (comer / vivir) |
|--------|------------|----------------------|
| yo | habl**aba** | com**ía** / viv**ía** |
| tú | habl**abas** | com**ías** / viv**ías** |
| él/ella | habl**aba** | com**ía** / viv**ía** |
| nosotros | habl**ábamos** | com**íamos** / viv**íamos** |
| vosotros | habl**abais** | com**íais** / viv**íais** |
| ellos | habl**aban** | com**ían** / viv**ían** |

### The three irregular imperfects (memorize these)

| Verb | yo | tú | él | nosotros | vosotros | ellos |
|------|----|----|----|----|---------|-------|
| *ser* | era | eras | era | éramos | erais | eran |
| *ir* | iba | ibas | iba | íbamos | ibais | iban |
| *ver* | veía | veías | veía | veíamos | veíais | veían |

That's it — all other verbs are perfectly regular in the imperfect.

## 📚 3. Preterite vs. Imperfect — The Decision Tree

This is the most important grammar decision in Spanish narrative. Use this framework:

### Use PRETERITE for:
- **Completed actions** with a defined endpoint: *Comí una pizza.* (I ate a pizza — done.)
- **Sequential actions** in a narrative: *Llegué, abrí la puerta y entré.* (I arrived, opened the door, and went in.)
- **Actions that interrupt** an ongoing state: *Dormía cuando sonó el teléfono.* (I was sleeping [imp] when the phone rang [pret].)
- **Changes of state** (began to feel, became, etc.): *De repente tuve miedo.* (Suddenly I became afraid.)
- **Actions that occurred a specific number of times**: *Fui al gimnasio tres veces.* (I went to the gym three times.)

### Use IMPERFECT for:
- **Ongoing/habitual past actions** ("used to," "would always"): *De niño comía mucho.* (As a child I used to eat a lot.)
- **Background/setting** in a narrative: *Hacía frío y llovía.* (It was cold and it was raining.)
- **Ongoing states** (mental, emotional, physical): *Estaba cansado. Quería dormir.* (I was tired. I wanted to sleep.)
- **Age in the past**: *Tenía diez años cuando aprendí a leer.* (I was ten years old when I learned to read.)
- **Time in the past**: *Eran las tres de la mañana.* (It was three in the morning.)
- **Parallel ongoing actions** (while two things were happening at the same time): *Mientras trabajaba, escuchaba música.* (While I was working, I was listening to music.)

### The classic narrative structure: Imperfect sets the scene, Preterite advances the plot

> *Era una noche oscura y tormentosa. Llovía sin parar. De repente, alguien llamó a la puerta.*
> (It was a dark and stormy night. It was raining non-stop. Suddenly, someone knocked at the door.)

*Era, llovía* = imperfect (background/setting). *llamó* = preterite (specific event that advances the story).

### Time expression clues

| Suggests PRETERITE | Suggests IMPERFECT |
|--------------------|-------------------|
| *ayer, anteayer* | *siempre, nunca* |
| *el lunes pasado* | *todos los días, cada semana* |
| *una vez, dos veces* | *de niño/a, cuando era joven* |
| *de repente, de pronto* | *mientras, cuando (ongoing)* |
| *por fin, al fin* | *frecuentemente, a menudo* |
| *esa tarde, ese día* | *antes, en aquella época* |

## 📚 4. Compound Past (Pretérito Perfecto Compuesto)

Formed with: **haber (present) + past participle**

| haber | *he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han* |
|------|---------------------------------|

**Past participles:** -AR → -ado | -ER/-IR → -ido

*He hablado* (I have spoken) | *Has comido* (You have eaten) | *Ha vivido* (He/she has lived)

**Irregular past participles (memorize):**
*hacer → hecho | decir → dicho | ver → visto | escribir → escrito | poner → puesto | volver → vuelto | abrir → abierto | morir → muerto | romper → roto | cubrir → cubierto*

### Compound past vs. preterite — regional difference

| Region | When to use Compound Past |
|--------|--------------------------|
| **Spain** | Recent past with connection to present; today's events: *Esta mañana he desayunado tarde.* |
| **Latin America** | Virtually interchangeable with preterite (in practice): *Esta mañana desayuné tarde.* |

In most of Latin America, the simple preterite dominates in speech even when Spain would use the compound. Both are understood everywhere.

## 📚 5. Pluperfect (Pretérito Pluscuamperfecto)

"Had done" — an action that happened BEFORE another past action.

Formed with: **haber (imperfect) + past participle**
*había, habías, había, habíamos, habíais, habían* + past participle

*Cuando llegué, ella ya se había ido.* (When I arrived, she had already left.)
*No habían comido.* (They hadn't eaten.)
*Había vivido en México antes de mudarse.* (He had lived in Mexico before moving.)

## ⚠️ 6. Common Errors

| Error | Wrong | Right |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Using preterite for habitual past | *Fui al gimnasio todos los días.* | ***Iba** al gimnasio todos los días.* |
| Using imperfect for completed action | *Comía una pizza anoche.* (ambiguous — sounds like "I was eating") | ***Comí** una pizza anoche.* |
| Wrong ser/ir preterite | *sería/iría* (that's conditional) | *fui/fue/fueron* |
| Forgetting spelling change in -car/-gar/-zar yo preterite | *Buscé, llegé* | *Busqué, llegué* |
