# 37.6 — Adjectives: i-adjectives vs na-adjectives

> *"Japanese adjectives conjugate like verbs — and once you see them that way, they stop being confusing."*

Japanese has two types of adjectives with fundamentally different grammatical behavior. **い-adjectives** (i-adjectives) conjugate by changing their ending — they behave almost like verbs. **な-adjectives** (na-adjectives) are more like nouns — they need the copula (です/だ) to connect to the sentence and な when directly modifying a noun. This chapter is short but essential — adjective errors are highly visible in speech.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

1. Classify any adjective as い-adjective or な-adjective.
2. Conjugate い-adjectives in all four core forms (present/past, affirmative/negative).
3. Use な-adjectives with the copula in all four core forms.
4. Use both adjective types to directly modify nouns.
5. Chain adjectives using the て-form of い-adjectives and な + で for な-adjectives.
6. Form comparatives and superlatives in Japanese.

## 📚 1. The Two Adjective Classes

| Class | Identifying feature | Examples |
|-------|---------------------|---------|
| **い-adjective** | Ends in い in dictionary form (preceded by a non-な sound) | 大きい (ookii = big), 小さい (chiisai = small), 高い (takai = tall/expensive), 安い (yasui = cheap), 面白い (omoshiroi = interesting), 難しい (muzukashii = difficult), 新しい (atarashii = new), 古い (furui = old), 良い/いい (yoi/ii = good) |
| **な-adjective** | Ends in な when directly before a noun; dictionary form usually ends in a non-い sound | 元気 (genki = energetic/healthy), 好き (suki = liked/favorite), 嫌い (kirai = disliked), きれい (kirei = clean/pretty), 静か (shizuka = quiet), 便利 (benri = convenient), 有名 (yuumei = famous), 大切 (taisetsu = important), 親切 (shinsetsu = kind) |

> **The い trap:** Several な-adjectives end in い: *きれい* (pretty), *嫌い* (disliked), *有名* (famous is *yuumei* but not ending in い). These must be memorized as な-adjectives despite the い ending. The test: can you attach な directly to modify a noun? *きれいな* → yes, it's な-adjective.

> **いい (good):** This is an い-adjective with an irregular conjugation — it conjugates as *よい* (yoi) in all forms except the dictionary form: *よくない, よかった, よくなかった* — never *いくない* or *いかった*.

---

## 📚 2. い-Adjective Conjugation

| Form | Rule | Example (大きい) | Example (良い→よい) |
|------|------|---------------|-----------------|
| Present affirmative | dictionary form (or + です) | 大きい / 大きいです | いい / いいです |
| Present negative | Remove い → くない (+ です) | 大きくない / 大きくないです | よくない |
| Past affirmative | Remove い → かった (+ です) | 大きかった / 大きかったです | よかった |
| Past negative | Remove い → くなかった (+ です) | 大きくなかった | よくなかった |

**Plain form summary:**
- Present: 大きい
- Negative: 大きくない
- Past: 大きかった
- Past negative: 大きくなかった

**Polite form:** Simply add です to the plain form: 大きいです / 大きくないです / 大きかったです / 大きくなかったです

> Note: Technically *大きいです* is sufficient — the です is added for politeness but the い-adjective already contains a complete predicate. *大きくないです* is colloquially common; the more formal form is *大きくありません*.

---

## 📚 3. な-Adjective Conjugation

な-adjectives use the copula (です/だ) for all conjugation — the adjective itself never changes:

| Form | Construction | Example (元気) |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| Present affirmative | Adj + です | 元気です |
| Present negative | Adj + じゃありません / ではありません | 元気じゃありません |
| Past affirmative | Adj + でした | 元気でした |
| Past negative | Adj + じゃありませんでした | 元気じゃありませんでした |

**Plain forms:**
- Present: 元気だ
- Negative: 元気じゃない
- Past: 元気だった
- Past negative: 元気じゃなかった

---

## 📚 4. Modifying Nouns Directly

Both adjective types can directly precede a noun. The connecting form differs:

| Adjective type | Before noun | Example |
|---------------|------------|---------|
| い-adjective | dictionary form (unchanged) | **大きい**犬 (a big dog) |
| な-adjective | Adj + **な** | **元気な**犬 (an energetic dog) |

*高い山* (a tall mountain — い-adj directly before noun)
*有名な山* (a famous mountain — な-adj + な before noun)
*きれいな花* (a beautiful flower — な-adj)
*新しいコンピューター* (a new computer — い-adj)

---

## 📚 5. Chaining Adjectives (て-form)

To connect multiple adjectives ("big and fast and expensive"), use:

**い-adjectives:** Remove い → く + て
*大きくて高い車* (a big and expensive car)
*むずかしくて面白い授業* (a difficult and interesting class)

**な-adjectives:** Adj + で
*きれいで静かな部屋* (a clean and quiet room)
*元気で親切な先生* (an energetic and kind teacher)

**Mixed:**
*新しくて便利なアプリ* (a new and convenient app — い then な)

## 📚 6. Comparatives and Superlatives

Japanese does NOT modify the adjective itself for comparison (no -er/-est). Instead, sentence structure carries the comparison:

### Comparative — "A is more ___ than B"
**B より A の方が [adjective]**

*このほうが あのほんより おもしろいです。*
(This one is more interesting than that book.)

*東京はおおさかより おおきいです。*
(Tokyo is bigger than Osaka.)

### Superlative — "A is the most ___ (in X)"
**X の中で A が一番 [adjective]**

*クラスの中で田中さんが一番背が高いです。*
(In the class, Tanaka-san is the tallest.)

*このレストランの中で、すしが一番おいしいです。*
(In this restaurant, sushi is the most delicious.)

### Asking which is more: どちら / どっち

*AとBと、どちらの方が [adjective] ですか？*
*コーヒーとお茶と、どちらの方が好きですか？*
(Which do you like more, coffee or tea?)

---

## 📚 7. Becoming — なる (to become)

Adjectives + なる = "to become ___"

**い-adjective:** Remove い → く + なる
*大きくなる* (to become big / to grow)
*日本語が上手くなりたい。* (I want to become good at Japanese.)

**な-adjective:** Adj + に + なる
*元気になる* (to become healthy)
*有名になりました。* (He became famous.)

## ⚠️ 9. Common Errors

| Error | Wrong | Right |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Conjugating いい as い-adj | いくない, いかった | **よく**ない, **よか**った |
| Missing な before noun | 元気犬 | 元気**な**犬 |
| Using な with い-adj | 大きな犬 | Wait — *大きな* actually exists as an archaic alternative! But conjugation is always い-adj style. |
| Wrong て-form chain | 高いで便利 | 高く**て**便利 (い-adj → くて) |
| Forgetting to use のほうが for comparison | 東京は大きいです (no comparison stated) | 東京はおおさかより大きいです |
