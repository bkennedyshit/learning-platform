# APP-05 — Civics & Government

*Part of [10 - Learning App Material](README)*

> **App context:** Required in most states for graduation. One of the most-taken AP exams. Civics literacy is foundational for understanding every political news cycle, every Supreme Court decision, and every election — arguably the most immediately applicable subject in the entire K-12 pipeline.

## 3. Chapter Outline

| # | Chapter | Core skill | AP Unit |
|---|---------|-----------|---------|
| 05.1 | **Foundations of American Democracy** | Enlightenment principles (Locke, Montesquieu, Rousseau); Articles of Confederation — why they failed; Constitutional Convention debates; federalism (division of power between national and state governments); the Madisonian model (separation of powers, checks and balances, bicameralism) | AP Unit 1 |
| 05.2 | **The Constitution — Structure & Principles** | The 7 Articles of the Constitution; the amendment process (Article V); federalism — enumerated, implied, reserved, concurrent powers; the Supremacy Clause; elastic clause; full faith and credit; the 27 Amendments with focus on 1st, 4th, 5th, 6th, 14th | AP Unit 1 |
| 05.3 | **Congress — Structure & Function** | Bicameralism rationale; House vs. Senate (size, terms, powers, representation); how a bill becomes a law (and all the ways it doesn't); congressional committees; congressional leadership; legislative strategies (filibuster, cloture, discharge petition) | AP Unit 2 |
| 05.4 | **The Presidency** | Constitutional powers (Article II); formal vs. informal powers; executive orders and executive privilege; the cabinet; the bureaucracy and its relationship to the President; the role of the President in the legislative process; war powers | AP Unit 2 |
| 05.5 | **The Federal Judiciary** | Article III courts; federal court structure (district → circuit → Supreme Court); judicial review (Marbury v. Madison); judicial philosophy (originalism vs. living constitution); landmark Supreme Court cases — Marbury, McCulloch, Gibbons, Dred Scott, Plessy, Brown, Gideon, Miranda, Roe/Dobbs, Obergefell | AP Unit 2 |
| 05.6 | **Civil Liberties & Civil Rights** | Bill of Rights incorporation (14th Amendment); 1st Amendment (speech, press, religion, assembly, petition) with key cases; 4th/5th/6th Amendment criminal procedure; equal protection (14th) and levels of scrutiny; civil rights movement and legislation (Civil Rights Act 1964, Voting Rights Act 1965) | AP Unit 3 |
| 05.7 | **Political Participation & Public Opinion** | Voting behavior and demographics; voter registration and turnout; political parties (origin, realignment, current coalitions); interest groups and lobbying; the media's role in democracy; campaigns and elections (Electoral College, campaign finance — Citizens United) | AP Unit 4-5 |
| 05.8 | **Public Policy & Policymaking** | Types of policy (domestic, economic, foreign, social); the policymaking process (agenda-setting → formulation → adoption → implementation → evaluation); fiscal policy (Congress/President) vs. monetary policy (Federal Reserve); foreign policy institutions (NSC, State Dept, DoD, CIA) | AP Unit 4-5 |
