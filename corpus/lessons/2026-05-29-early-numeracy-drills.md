## Chapter 02.1 — Number Bonds to 10 and 20 (Seed: 42)

> Solve each problem. For Ten-Frame drills, count the asterisks (*) and find the number needed to fill the frames.

### 📝 Question 1
We have 2 dots in our Ten-Frame.
  [ * * . . . ]
  [ . . . . . ]
How many MORE dots do we need to make a WHOLE of 10?

?

**Answer:**
`8`

**Explanation:**
Ten-Frame check: 2 dots present, 8 empty cells. Since 2 + 8 = 10, the missing part is 8.
