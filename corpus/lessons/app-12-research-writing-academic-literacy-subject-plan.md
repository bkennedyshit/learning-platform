*Part of [10 - Learning App Material](README)*

> **App context:** Research Writing is the one skill that transfers to every other subject in the platform. Every AP free-response question, every college paper, every lab report is a writing task. This track is positioned as a cross-cutting skill layer — it makes every other track more productive.

## 3. Chapter Outline

| # | Chapter | Core skill |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 12.1 | **The Writing Process** | Prewriting (brainstorming, freewriting, outlining, mind-mapping); drafting without self-editing; revision (big-picture: thesis, structure, argument) vs. editing (surface: grammar, style, clarity); peer review strategies; the writing process as recursive (not linear) |
| 12.2 | **Thesis & Argument Construction** | What a thesis is (not a statement of fact; a debatable claim + reason); strong vs. weak thesis statements; the three types of claims (factual, interpretive, evaluative); Toulmin model of argument (claim → warrant → backing → qualifier → rebuttal); logical fallacies (ad hominem, straw man, false dichotomy, slippery slope, appeal to authority) |
| 12.3 | **Evidence, Analysis & Integration** | Types of evidence (statistical, anecdotal, expert authority, textual); evaluating source credibility (CRAAP test: Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose); integrating evidence (quote, paraphrase, summary + citation); avoiding plagiarism; the ACE method (Assert → Cite → Explain) |
| 12.4 | **Research Skills & Source Evaluation** | Library databases vs. Google; Google Scholar search operators; evaluating websites (.edu/.gov vs. .com); peer-reviewed vs. popular sources; primary vs. secondary vs. tertiary sources; annotated bibliography; research question formulation; systematic note-taking |
| 12.5 | **Citation & Documentation** | MLA 9th edition (in-text citation, Works Cited format for books/articles/websites/databases); APA 7th edition (in-text citation, References format); Chicago/Turabian (footnotes vs. author-date); when to cite; what does and doesn't require citation; using Zotero for citation management |
| 12.6 | **Essay Structures & Genres** | Argumentative essay (classical structure: intro → background → thesis → body → counterargument → rebuttal → conclusion); comparative essay; analytical essay; expository essay; the literature review (how to synthesize multiple sources into a coherent narrative); writing for different audiences |
| 12.7 | **Disciplinary Writing** | Scientific writing (IMRaD structure: Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion); social science writing (APA format; literature review structure; methodology section); humanities writing (MLA, close reading, textual analysis); business writing (executive summary, memo, email); lab reports |
| 12.8 | **Advanced Rhetoric & Style** | Rhetorical situation (author, audience, purpose, context); rhetorical appeals (ethos, pathos, logos, kairos); visual rhetoric (analyzing images, infographics, advertisements); style and voice (concision, active vs. passive voice, sentence variety); AP Lang FRQ formats (synthesis essay, rhetorical analysis, argument) |

## 4. App Integration

Research Writing is embedded throughout the platform:
