# APP-07 — Art History

*Part of [10 - Learning App Material](README)*

> **App context:** AP Art History is the most visual AP course and the hardest to teach without images. The platform's advantage here is direct: embed high-quality images with interactive zoom, cross-link to historical context in Track 18 (World History), and build image-identification drills the way the anatomy track builds label-blank drills.

## 3. Chapter Outline

| # | Chapter | Core skill | AP Content Area |
|---|---------|-----------|----------------|
| 07.1 | **Visual Analysis & Art Historical Method** | Formal analysis (line, shape, color, texture, space, composition); iconography; stylistic analysis; contextual analysis; primary vs. secondary sources in art history; writing about art (description vs. analysis vs. interpretation) | Cross-cutting skill |
| 07.2 | **Global Prehistory & Ancient Mediterranean** | Prehistoric art (Lascaux, Stonehenge, Cycladic); Ancient Near East (Uruk, Akkadian, Babylonian, Assyrian, Persian); Ancient Egypt (Old/Middle/New Kingdom — canon, hierarchy of scale, frontalism); Aegean (Minoan, Mycenaean) | AP Units 1-2 |
| 07.3 | **Ancient Greece & Rome** | Greek Archaic (kouros/kore) → Classical (Parthenon, Riace Warriors, Discobolus) → Hellenistic (Laocoön, Nike of Samothrace); Roman architecture (orders, concrete, Pantheon, Colosseum); Roman portraiture and narrative relief | AP Unit 2 |
| 07.4 | **Early Christian, Byzantine & Islamic Art** | Catacomb paintings; Early Christian basilica plan; Byzantine mosaics (Ravenna, Hagia Sophia); icon painting; Islamic architecture (mosque plan, arabesque, calligraphy — Dome of the Rock, Alhambra, Great Mosque of Córdoba) | AP Unit 3 |
| 07.5 | **Medieval & Renaissance Europe** | Romanesque and Gothic architecture (pointed arch, flying buttress, stained glass — Chartres); Proto-Renaissance (Cimabue, Duccio, Giotto); Italian Renaissance (Masaccio, Botticelli, Leonardo, Michelangelo, Raphael); Northern Renaissance (Van Eyck, Dürer, Bosch) | AP Unit 5 |
| 07.6 | **Baroque, Rococo & Neoclassicism** | Counter-Reformation Baroque (Caravaggio, Bernini, Rubens, Rembrandt, Vermeer); French Baroque (Le Brun, Versailles); Rococo (Watteau, Fragonard); Enlightenment Neoclassicism (David, Canova); Romanticism (Géricault, Delacroix, Turner, Friedrich) | AP Unit 6 |
| 07.7 | **19th & Early 20th Century** | Realism (Courbet, Daumier); Impressionism (Monet, Degas, Renoir) and Post-Impressionism (Cézanne, Van Gogh, Gauguin, Seurat); Fauvism (Matisse); Expressionism; Cubism (Picasso, Braque); Dada; Surrealism (Dalí, Magritte); Abstraction (Kandinsky, Mondrian) | AP Unit 7 |
| 07.8 | **Contemporary & Global Art** | Abstract Expressionism (Pollock, de Kooning); Pop Art (Warhol, Lichtenstein); Minimalism; Conceptual Art; Postmodernism; Global contemporary (African, Latin American, Asian, Indigenous perspectives in modern art); installation, performance, digital art | AP Unit 8 |

## 4. App-Specific Feature: Image Drill

The image-identification drill for art history works like the anatomy label-blank drill:
- Show an artwork image → student identifies title, artist, date, culture, medium, and one formal analysis point
- Configurable: spaced repetition mode (Anki-style), timed identification, multiple choice
- All 250 AP Art History required works need to be in the drill database
