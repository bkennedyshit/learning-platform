# Subject Syllabus: 16 - Algebra II & Pre-Calculus

*Back to [Learning Index](00---09---Learning-Index) · [9-12 Pipeline](9-12-High-School-Pipeline)*

Algebra II and Pre-Calculus together form the **runway to Calculus** — the course that opens all STEM doors. The material in this track is harder than Algebra I but more beautiful: you'll discover that polynomials have deep structure, that exponential and logarithmic functions are inverses of each other, that the unit circle encodes the geometry of all oscillation, and that limits provide the first glimpse of how calculus works. Every chapter in this track is a direct prerequisite for something important in higher mathematics.

## 📝 3. Chapter Outline

| # | Chapter | Core skill |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 16.1 | **Polynomial Functions** | Remainder theorem; factor theorem; rational root theorem; synthetic division; polynomial long division; end behavior and leading coefficient test; multiplicity of zeros; graphing polynomials; Descartes' rule of signs; complex roots (intro: a + bi). |
| 16.2 | **Rational Functions** | Domain; vertical asymptotes (zeros of denominator); horizontal asymptotes (degree comparison); oblique/slant asymptotes (long division); holes in the graph; graphing rational functions; partial fraction decomposition (intro). |
| 16.3 | **Exponential & Logarithmic Functions** | Exponential growth/decay; the number e; natural exponential function eˣ; definition of logarithm; log properties (product, quotient, power, change of base); natural log ln; solving exponential and logarithmic equations; half-life and compound interest applications. Cross-link: [01.5 - Differential Equations](01.5---Differential-Equations) |
| 16.4 | **Trigonometry — The Unit Circle** | Angle measure (degrees and radians — exact conversions); the unit circle (all 16 special angles — memorize); six trig functions (sin, cos, tan, csc, sec, cot) defined on the unit circle; reference angles; signs of trig functions by quadrant; trig values of special angles from memory. Cross-link: [46.6 - Right Triangles & Trig](46.6---Right-Triangles-&-Trig) |
| 16.5 | **Trig Identities & Equations** | Reciprocal, quotient, Pythagorean identities (sin²+cos²=1 family); co-function identities; even/odd identities; sum and difference formulas (sin/cos/tan of A±B); double-angle formulas; half-angle formulas; solving trig equations (general solutions); Law of Sines and Law of Cosines (full treatment). |
| 16.6 | **Conic Sections** | Parabola (focus, directrix, latus rectum, standard form); ellipse (foci, vertices, major/minor axes, eccentricity); hyperbola (foci, vertices, asymptotes, eccentricity); circle; identifying conics from general form Ax²+Bxy+Cy²+Dx+Ey+F=0; parametric equations intro. |
| 16.7 | **Sequences, Series & the Binomial Theorem** | Arithmetic sequences/series; geometric sequences/series; sigma notation; convergent vs. divergent infinite geometric series; binomial theorem (Pascal's triangle, combinations nCr, binomial expansion). Cross-link: [01.2 - Analysis & Calculus](01.2---Analysis-&-Calculus) |
| 16.8 | **Limits & Introduction to Calculus** | Intuitive concept of a limit; evaluating limits graphically and algebraically; one-sided limits; limits at infinity; continuity (definition, types of discontinuity); average rate of change vs. instantaneous rate of change; introduction to the derivative as a limit. Cross-link: [01.2 - Analysis & Calculus](01.2---Analysis-&-Calculus) |
