## 3. Chapter Outline

| # | Chapter | Core skill | AP Unit |
|---|---------|-----------|---------|
| 09.1 | **History & Research Methods** | Schools of psychology (structuralism, functionalism, behaviorism, psychoanalysis, humanism, cognitive, neuroscience, sociocultural); research methods (experiment — IV/DV/controls; survey; case study; naturalistic observation); statistics in psychology (descriptive and inferential); ethical guidelines (APA ethics code) | AP Unit 1 |
| 09.2 | **Biological Bases of Behavior** | Neurons (structure and function); neurotransmitters (dopamine, serotonin, GABA, acetylcholine — and their roles); brain structures and their functions (lobes, limbic system, brainstem, cerebellum); hemispheric lateralization; split-brain research; genetics and behavior (heritability, twin studies); endocrine system | AP Unit 2 |
| 09.3 | **Sensation & Perception** | Sensation vs. perception; transduction; absolute and difference thresholds; signal detection theory; sensory adaptation; perceptual principles (Gestalt — figure/ground, proximity, similarity, closure, continuity); depth cues (monocular and binocular); perceptual constancy; top-down vs. bottom-up processing | AP Unit 3 |
| 09.4 | **States of Consciousness** | Sleep stages (NREM 1-3 and REM) and their functions; sleep disorders (insomnia, sleep apnea, narcolepsy, night terrors); dreams (Freudian vs. activation-synthesis); hypnosis; psychoactive drugs (depressants, stimulants, hallucinogens, opioids — mechanisms and effects); meditation | AP Unit 3 |
| 09.5 | **Learning & Conditioning** | Classical conditioning (Pavlov — CS, UCS, UCR, CR; acquisition, extinction, spontaneous recovery, generalization, discrimination); operant conditioning (Skinner — reinforcement schedules, punishment, shaping, chaining); observational learning (Bandura — Bobo doll); cognitive maps; learned helplessness | AP Unit 4 |
| 09.6 | **Cognition, Memory & Language** | Information processing model (encoding → storage → retrieval); memory types (sensory, short-term/working, long-term); encoding strategies; retrieval cues; forgetting (decay, interference, repression); memory errors (misinformation effect — Loftus); problem-solving; heuristics and biases (Kahneman — availability, representativeness, anchoring); language acquisition | AP Unit 5-6 |
| 09.7 | **Development, Motivation & Emotion** | Developmental theories (Piaget's cognitive stages, Erikson's psychosocial stages, Kohlberg's moral development, Vygotsky's ZPD); attachment (Harlow, Ainsworth); adolescence and identity (Marcia); motivation theories (Maslow's hierarchy, drive-reduction, intrinsic vs. extrinsic); emotion theories (James-Lange, Cannon-Bard, Schachter-Singer two-factor) | AP Unit 6-7 |
| 09.8 | **Personality, Social Psychology & Abnormal** | Psychoanalytic theory (Freud — id/ego/superego, defense mechanisms); trait theories (Big Five personality model — OCEAN); humanistic (Maslow, Rogers); social influence (Milgram obedience, Zimbardo prison study, Asch conformity); cognitive dissonance; attribution theory; psychological disorders (DSM-5 categories — anxiety, mood, schizophrenia spectrum, personality disorders); treatment approaches | AP Unit 7-9 |
