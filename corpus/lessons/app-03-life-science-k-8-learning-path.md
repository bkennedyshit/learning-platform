## Recommended Chapter Order

Follow chapters 03.1 → 03.8 in sequence. Each chapter builds on the previous.

| Phase | Chapters | Deliverable |
|-------|---------|------------|
| **Phase 1 — Foundation** | 03.1 – 03.3 | Core conceptual literacy; can explain the big ideas without notes |
| **Phase 2 — Application** | 03.4 – 03.6 | Problem-solving and analysis; 20+ practice items per chapter |
| **Phase 3 — Synthesis** | 03.7 – 03.8 | Full-length practice exam; real-world or AP-style application |

---

## Daily Study Habits

| Habit | Time | Why |
|-------|------|-----|
| Active retrieval (never passive re-reading) | 20-30 min | Retrieval practice is the highest-leverage learning activity |
| Review errors from previous session | 5-10 min | Errors are the most efficient learning signal |
| Platform drill script or Khan Academy exercises | 10-15 min | Spaced interleaved practice beats blocked practice |
