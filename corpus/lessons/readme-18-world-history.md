## 📚 Authoritative Catalogs & Sources

_Pulled verbatim from `Subject_Plan.md §2 — Free Learning Catalog`. Source of truth is the Subject_Plan; this section is a convenience copy._

- **[Crash Course World History (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@crashcourse)** — John Green's 45-episode series; entertaining, dense, excellent; **the best first-pass for every chapter**
- **[Heimler's History (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@heimlershistory)** — AP World History review; clear thematic analysis; exam skills
- **[Khan Academy AP World History](https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/ap-world-history)** — structured practice aligned to AP exam
- **[World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/)** — free, peer-reviewed articles on every civilization; use as a reference for every chapter
- **[WHAP (World History for AP) — Marco Learning (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@MarcoLearning)** — AP exam prep; short thematic videos
- **[OpenStax World History (Volume 1 & 2)](https://openstax.org/details/books/world-history-volume-1)** — free, college-level world history textbook ← **primary text**
- **[Google Arts & Culture](https://artsandculture.google.com/)** — primary source images from every civilization and period; excellent for document-based questions
- **[Library of Congress — World Digital Library](https://www.wdl.org/en/)** — free primary sources from every continent and century
