## Pedagogical Philosophy for K-5

K-5 learners are **not small adults** — their cognitive architecture is fundamentally different:

- **Concrete before abstract:** Every concept must be introduced with physical/visual anchors before symbolic representation. Fractions = pizza slices before fractions = numerator/denominator.
- **Phonics-first literacy:** Systematic phonics (letter-sound mapping) is the most evidence-based reading instruction method. The whole-language approach (memorize words by sight) produces worse outcomes for most learners.
- **Spaced practice over massed practice:** 10 min/day beats 70 min/week. Daily short sessions are non-negotiable at this age.
- **Story and narrative:** Social studies, science, and history should be delivered through stories. Narrative memory is the most durable memory system at this age.
- **Active recall from day one:** Even K learners can do "turn and tell" (retrieval practice with a partner). Don't wait until high school to introduce active recall.

## Subject 1: Early Literacy & Writing (Track 53)

### Grade-by-Grade Scope

| Grade | Literacy | Writing |
|-------|---------|---------|
| **K** | Phonemic awareness (oral), letter-sound correspondence (26 phonemes), CVC words, sight words (Dolch pre-primer) | Name writing, letter formation, dictation |
| **1** | Blending/segmenting 3-4 phoneme words, short vowel patterns, consonant blends/digraphs, sight words (Dolch primer + 1st grade) | Simple sentences, capital/period, personal narrative |
| **2** | Long vowel patterns (CVCe, vowel teams), r-controlled vowels, fluency building (60-80 WCPM), Dolch 2nd grade | 2-3 sentence paragraphs, opinion writing intro |
| **3** | Multisyllabic decoding, prefixes/suffixes, reading comprehension strategies (main idea, inference), Dolch 3rd grade | 5-sentence paragraph, informative writing |
| **4** | Vocabulary (context clues, word roots), literary elements (character, plot, theme), nonfiction features | Multi-paragraph essays, narrative arc |
| **5** | Point of view, author's purpose, summarizing, compare/contrast, text structures | 5-paragraph essay, research writing intro, citations |

### Key Concepts by Domain

**Phonics sequence (K-2 — the critical window):**
1. Consonants + short vowels → CVC words (cat, dog, sit)
2. Consonant blends (bl, cr, st) + digraphs (sh, ch, th, wh)
3. Long vowels: CVCe (cake, bike, hope), vowel teams (rain, play, read, boat)
4. R-controlled vowels (ar, er, ir, or, ur)
5. Diphthongs (oi, oy, ou, ow) + other patterns
6. Multisyllabic words: compound words → open/closed syllables → prefixes/suffixes

**Reading comprehension (grades 3-5):**
- Main idea + supporting details
- Making inferences from evidence in text
- Summarizing vs. retelling
- Compare/contrast across texts
- Author's purpose (PIE: Persuade, Inform, Entertain)
- Text structure: chronological, cause/effect, compare/contrast, problem/solution, description

## Subject 2: Early Numeracy / K-5 Mathematics (Track 57)

### Grade-by-Grade Scope

| Grade | Number & Operations | Measurement | Geometry | Other |
|-------|-------------------|-------------|---------|-------|
| **K** | Counting 0-20, comparing numbers, addition/subtraction within 10 | Direct comparison (longer/shorter/heavier) | 2D/3D shapes identification | — |
| **1** | Add/subtract within 20, place value to 120, add/subtract 2-digit | Ordering 3 objects, measuring with non-standard units | Composing/decomposing shapes | — |
| **2** | Add/subtract within 1000, place value to 1000, skip counting | Standard measurement (ruler, scale), telling time | Partitioning shapes into halves/thirds | Data: picture graphs |
| **3** | Multiply/divide within 100, fractions ½ ⅓ ¼, rounding | Area and perimeter, liquid volume, mass | Quadrilaterals | Data: bar graphs, line plots |
| **4** | Multi-digit multiplication/division, equivalent fractions, decimal notation, ordering fractions | Angle measurement, unit conversion | Lines, angles, symmetry | — |
| **5** | Multiply/divide fractions, decimal operations, coordinate plane intro | Volume of rectangular prisms | 2D shapes classification | Data: line plots with fractions |

### Key Conceptual Milestones

**The critical K-5 math skills (each one unlocks the next):**
1. **Subitizing** (K) — instantly recognize quantities to 5 without counting. Foundation of number sense.
2. **Place value understanding** (1-3) — 10 ones = 1 ten = place-value system. The most foundational concept in arithmetic.
3. **Multiplication as equal groups** (3) — NOT just memorizing tables; understanding 4×3 = 4 groups of 3.
4. **Fractions as numbers on a number line** (3-4) — NOT just "parts of a shape." This is where most US students develop persistent misconceptions.
5. **Division as finding an unknown factor** (4-5) — 24 ÷ 6 = ? → 6 × ? = 24
6. **Decimal-fraction connection** (4-5) — 0.3 = 3/10; building toward ratio/proportion in grade 6.

## App Delivery Notes for K-5

K-5 content requires different UX than the rest of the platform:

- **No walls of text.** Lessons must be presented in chunks of ≤150 words with supporting visuals.
- **Audio support.** Many K-2 learners cannot read lesson text independently. All content needs text-to-speech or read-aloud functionality.
- **Parental/guardian interface.** Progress tracking goes to a parent dashboard, not to the child.
- **Short sessions.** Max session length: 10-15 min for K-2; 15-20 min for 3-5.
- **No gamification traps.** Points/badges are fine as acknowledgment; never gate content behind them.
- **Print-friendly.** Many K-5 activities need to be printable (coloring pages, mazes, graphic organizers).
