# 43.4 — The Verb System: Present System Active & Passive

*Back to [Subject_Plan](Subject_Plan) | [← 43.3](43.3---Adjectives,-Pronouns-&-Agreement) | [43.5 →](43.5---The-Perfect-System-&-Infinitives)*

> **Chapter goal:** Master all four Latin conjugations and key irregular verbs in the three present-system tenses (present, imperfect, future) in both active and passive voice — and understand the agent construction.

---

## 1. Latin Verb Anatomy

Every Latin verb form encodes five pieces of information:
**Person** (1st/2nd/3rd) × **Number** (singular/plural) × **Tense** (present/imperfect/future/perfect/pluperfect/future perfect) × **Mood** (indicative/subjunctive/imperative) × **Voice** (active/passive)

### Principal Parts

Every Latin verb requires **four principal parts** to be fully specified:

| Part | Form | Information |
|------|------|------------|
| 1st | 1st sg. present active indicative | Present stem |
| 2nd | Present active infinitive | Confirms conjugation |
| 3rd | 1st sg. perfect active indicative | Perfect stem |
| 4th | Perfect passive participle (supine) | 4th principal part stem |

Example: *amō, amāre, amāvī, amātum* — to love

### The Four Conjugations

| Conjugation | Infinitive ending | Present stem vowel | Example |
|------------|-----------------|-------------------|---------|
| **1st** | *-āre* | -ā- | *amō, amāre* (love) |
| **2nd** | *-ēre* (long ē) | -ē- | *moneō, monēre* (warn) |
| **3rd** | *-ere* (short e) | -e-/-i- | *agō, agere* (drive/do) |
| **3rd -iō** | *-ere* (short e) | -i- | *capiō, capere* (take) |
| **4th** | *-īre* | -ī- | *audiō, audīre* (hear) |

> **3rd vs. 4th is the hardest distinction:** Both exist. 3rd conjugation has short *-e-* in the infinitive; 4th has long *-ī-*. The 3rd -iō subtype (*capiō, capere*) has -i- in many forms like the 4th but follows 3rd endings.

## 2. Personal Endings

### Active Personal Endings

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|--------|---------|--------|
| 1st | **-ō** (present) / **-m** (other tenses) | **-mus** |
| 2nd | **-s** | **-tis** |
| 3rd | **-t** | **-nt** |

### Passive Personal Endings

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|--------|---------|--------|
| 1st | **-r** | **-mur** |
| 2nd | **-ris** | **-minī** |
| 3rd | **-tur** | **-ntur** |

## 3. Present Tense — All Four Conjugations

### Active

| Person | 1st (*amō*) | 2nd (*moneō*) | 3rd (*agō*) | 3rd -iō (*capiō*) | 4th (*audiō*) |
|--------|------------|--------------|------------|-----------------|--------------|
| sg. 1 | am**ō** | mone**ō** | ag**ō** | capi**ō** | audi**ō** |
| sg. 2 | amā**s** | monē**s** | agi**s** | capi**s** | audī**s** |
| sg. 3 | ama**t** | mone**t** | agi**t** | capi**t** | audi**t** |
| pl. 1 | amā**mus** | monē**mus** | agi**mus** | capi**mus** | audī**mus** |
| pl. 2 | amā**tis** | monē**tis** | agi**tis** | capi**tis** | audī**tis** |
| pl. 3 | ama**nt** | mone**nt** | agu**nt** | capiu**nt** | audiu**nt** |

### Passive

| Person | 1st (*amor*) | 2nd (*moneor*) | 3rd (*agor*) | 4th (*audior*) |
|--------|-------------|---------------|-------------|--------------|
| sg. 1 | am**or** | mone**or** | ag**or** | audi**or** |
| sg. 2 | amā**ris** | monē**ris** | ager**is** | audī**ris** |
| sg. 3 | amā**tur** | monē**tur** | agi**tur** | audī**tur** |
| pl. 1 | amā**mur** | monē**mur** | agi**mur** | audī**mur** |
| pl. 2 | ama**minī** | mone**minī** | agi**minī** | audī**minī** |
| pl. 3 | ama**ntur** | mone**ntur** | agu**ntur** | audiu**ntur** |

## 4. Imperfect Tense — "was doing / used to do / kept doing"

**Formation:** present stem + **tense sign -bā-** + personal endings

| Conj. | Tense sign | 1st sg. | 3rd pl. |
|-------|-----------|---------|---------|
| 1st | -**bā**- | *amābam* | *amābant* |
| 2nd | -**bā**- | *monēbam* | *monēbant* |
| 3rd | -**bā**- | *agēbam* | *agēbant* |
| 3rd -iō | -**bā**- | *capiēbam* | *capiēbant* |
| 4th | -**bā**- | *audiēbam* | *audiēbant* |

Full imperfect active (1st conj., *amō*):
*amābam, amābas, amābat, amābāmus, amābātis, amābant*

Passive: replace active endings with passive endings after -bā-:
*amābar, amābāris, amābātur, amābāmur, amābāminī, amābantur*

---

## 5. Future Tense — "will do"

**Two different formation patterns — the most error-prone feature of Latin tenses:**

### 1st and 2nd Conjugation Future: stem + **-bi-/-bo-/-bu-** + endings

| Person | 1st (*amō*) | 2nd (*moneō*) |
|--------|------------|--------------|
| sg. 1 | amā**bō** | monē**bō** |
| sg. 2 | amā**bis** | monē**bis** |
| sg. 3 | amā**bit** | monē**bit** |
| pl. 1 | amā**bimus** | monē**bimus** |
| pl. 2 | amā**bitis** | monē**bitis** |
| pl. 3 | amā**bunt** | monē**bunt** |

### 3rd, 3rd -iō, and 4th Conjugation Future: stem + **-e-/-a-** + endings

| Person | 3rd (*agō*) | 3rd -iō (*capiō*) | 4th (*audiō*) |
|--------|------------|-----------------|--------------|
| sg. 1 | ag**am** | capi**am** | audi**am** |
| sg. 2 | ag**ēs** | capi**ēs** | audi**ēs** |
| sg. 3 | ag**et** | capi**et** | audi**et** |
| pl. 1 | ag**ēmus** | capi**ēmus** | audi**ēmus** |
| pl. 2 | ag**ētis** | capi**ētis** | audi**ētis** |
| pl. 3 | ag**ent** | capi**ent** | audi**ent** |

> ⚠️ **The critical trap:** 3rd conj. future 1st sg. *agam* looks exactly like 1st/2nd conj. present subjunctive forms. Context and knowledge of conjugation group disambiguate.

## 6. Irregular Verbs — Present System

### *esse* — to be (most important verb)

| Tense | sg. 1 | sg. 2 | sg. 3 | pl. 1 | pl. 2 | pl. 3 |
|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| Present | sum | es | est | sumus | estis | sunt |
| Imperfect | eram | eras | erat | erāmus | erātis | erant |
| Future | erō | eris | erit | erimus | eritis | erunt |

### *posse* — to be able (= *pot-* + *esse*)

Present: *possum, potes, potest, possumus, potestis, possunt*
Imperfect: *poteram, poterās...* Future: *poterō, poteris...*

### *īre* — to go

Present: *eō, īs, it, īmus, ītis, eunt*
Imperfect: *ībam, ībās, ībat, ībāmus, ībātis, ībant*
Future: *ībō, ībis, ībit, ībimus, ībitis, ībunt*

### *ferre* — to carry/bear (highly irregular)

Present active: *ferō, fers, fert, ferimus, fertis, ferunt*
Present passive: *feror, ferris, fertur, ferimur, feriminī, feruntur*

### *velle, nōlle, mālle* — to want / not want / prefer

*velle* present: *volō, vīs, vult, volumus, vultis, volunt*
*nōlle* present: *nōlō, nōn vīs, nōn vult, nōlumus, nōn vultis, nōlunt*
*mālle* present: *mālō, māvīs, māvult, mālumus, māvultis, mālunt*

## 7. The Passive Voice — Grammar & Constructions

### Ablative of Agent vs. Ablative of Means

| Construction | Used with | Case | Preposition | Example |
|-------------|----------|------|------------|---------|
| **Agent** | Person performing action | Ablative | **ā/ab** | *Puella ā servō amātur.* — The girl is loved by the slave. |
| **Means/instrument** | Thing used to perform action | Ablative | none | *Gladiō pugnant.* — They fight with/by means of a sword. |

> *ā/ab* + ablative = agent (living). No preposition = means/instrument (non-living).

### Passive Infinitive

Present passive infinitive: replace final *-e* of active infinitive with **-ī** (1st–3rd), or add **-rī** (4th):
*amāre → amārī / monēre → monērī / agere → agī / capere → capī / audīre → audīrī*

## 8. The Imperative

### Present Imperative Active

| | Singular | Plural |
|--|---------|--------|
| 1st conj. | *amā!* | *amāte!* |
| 2nd conj. | *monē!* | *monēte!* |
| 3rd conj. | *age!* | *agite!* |
| 3rd -iō | *cape!* | *capite!* |
| 4th conj. | *audī!* | *audīte!* |
| *esse* | *es!* | *este!* |

**Negative imperative:** *nōlī* (sg.) / *nōlīte* (pl.) + infinitive:
*Nōlī hoc facere!* — Don't do this! *Nōlīte currere!* — Don't run!

## 9. Common Misconceptions / Where Students Fail

1. **Confusing 3rd conj. future and present subjunctive:** *agam* can be future indicative (3rd conj.) or present subjunctive (1st/2nd conj.). Knowing which conjugation the verb belongs to resolves this.
2. **Applying the -bō/-bis/-bit future to 3rd/4th conjugation verbs:** *"audibō"* → wrong. 3rd and 4th use *-am/-ēs/-et* future.
3. **Forgetting *esse* irregulars:** *sum, es, est* — none of these look like regular verb forms. Must be memorised cold.
4. **Using preposition with ablative of means:** *"ā gladiō"* for a thing → wrong. Only persons take *ā/ab*. Instruments use bare ablative.
5. **Imperfect passive:** Some students apply active imperfect endings to passive. The passive has its own endings: *-bar, -bāris, -bātur, -bāmur, -bāminī, -bantur*.
