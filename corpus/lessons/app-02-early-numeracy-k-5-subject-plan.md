## 4. Key Conceptual Milestones (in order)

1. **Subitizing** (K) — instantly see quantities without counting; foundation of number sense
2. **Place value** (1-2) — 10 ones = 1 ten; the entire number system rests on this
3. **Multiplication as equal groups** (3) — not just memorized tables; structural understanding
4. **Fractions as numbers on a number line** (3-4) — the most misunderstood concept in K-5; most US students exit 5th grade thinking ½ is "part of a shape" not "a point between 0 and 1"
5. **Decimal-fraction connection** (4-5) — 0.3 = 3/10; builds toward ratio/proportion/percent
