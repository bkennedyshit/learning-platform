## Recommended Chapter Order

Follow chapters 10.1 → 10.8 in sequence. Each chapter builds on the previous.

| Phase | Chapters | Deliverable |
|-------|---------|------------|
| **Phase 1 — Foundation** | 10.1 – 10.3 | Core conceptual literacy; can explain the big ideas without notes |
| **Phase 2 — Application** | 10.4 – 10.6 | Problem-solving and analysis; 20+ practice items per chapter |
| **Phase 3 — Synthesis** | 10.7 – 10.8 | Full-length practice exam; real-world or AP-style application |

| Habit | Time | Why |
