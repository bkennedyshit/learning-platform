# 36.4 — Verb System & Present Tense

> *"The Spanish verb is a finite-state machine. Classify the verb, identify the tense and person, apply the rule."*

The Spanish verb system is the engine of the language. Unlike English, which uses auxiliary verbs (*I am going, I have gone, I will go*), Spanish encodes tense, mood, aspect, and person directly into the verb ending. This chapter covers the full conjugation framework and achieves fluency with the present tense — including the irregular verbs that appear in every sentence.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter you will be able to:

1. Classify any infinitive as -AR, -ER, or -IR and apply the correct present tense endings.
2. Conjugate the major irregular verbs in the present: *ser, estar, ir, tener, hacer, decir, poder, querer, saber, conocer*.
3. Identify and apply the four types of stem-changing verbs (e→ie, e→i, o→ue, u→ue).
4. Master the *ser* vs. *estar* distinction with all major use cases.
5. Form affirmative and negative sentences and yes/no questions in the present tense.

---

## 📚 1. The Verb Conjugation System

Every Spanish verb has:
- An **infinitive** form ending in *-ar*, *-er*, or *-ir*
- A **stem** (drop the infinitive ending) that carries the meaning
- **Endings** that encode: tense, mood, aspect, person, and number

**The six persons:**

| | Singular | Plural |
|--|---------|--------|
| 1st | *yo* | *nosotros/nosotras* |
| 2nd | *tú* (informal), *usted* (formal) | *vosotros/vosotras* (Spain), *ustedes* |
| 3rd | *él/ella* | *ellos/ellas* |

> Usted/ustedes conjugate **identically to él/ella/ellos/ellas** — 3rd person forms. This is why context matters; in formal address you use 3rd-person forms to mean "you."

---

## 📚 2. Regular Present Tense Endings

Remove the infinitive ending (-ar, -er, -ir), then add:

| Person | -AR (hablar) | -ER (comer) | -IR (vivir) |
|--------|------------|------------|------------|
| yo | habl**o** | com**o** | viv**o** |
| tú | habl**as** | com**es** | viv**es** |
| él/ella/Ud | habl**a** | com**e** | viv**e** |
| nosotros | habl**amos** | com**emos** | viv**imos** |
| vosotros | habl**áis** | com**éis** | viv**ís** |
| ellos/Uds | habl**an** | com**en** | viv**en** |

> **Pattern note:** -ER and -IR share almost identical endings in present tense — they diverge more in other tenses. The *yo* form across all three conjugations ends in *-o* for regular verbs.

## 📚 3. Stem-Changing Verbs

Some verbs change their stem vowel in all forms **except nosotros and vosotros** (the "boot" pattern — picture the conjugation grid as a boot).

### E → IE

| Verb | Yo | Tú | Él | Nos | Vos | Ellos |
|------|----|----|----|----|-----|-------|
| *pensar* (to think) | **pienso** | **piensas** | **piensa** | pensamos | pensáis | **piensan** |
| *querer* (to want) | **quiero** | **quieres** | **quiere** | queremos | queréis | **quieren** |
| *entender* (to understand) | **entiendo** | — | — | entendemos | — | — |
| *venir* (to come) | **vengo** | **vienes** | **viene** | venimos | venís | **vienen** |

Other e→ie: *cerrar, comenzar, despertar, perder, preferir, recomendar, sentir, tener*

### O → UE

| Verb | Yo | Tú | Él | Nos | Vos | Ellos |
|------|----|----|----|----|-----|-------|
| *poder* (to be able to) | **puedo** | **puedes** | **puede** | podemos | podéis | **pueden** |
| *dormir* (to sleep) | **duermo** | **duermes** | **duerme** | dormimos | dormís | **duermen** |
| *volver* (to return) | **vuelvo** | **vuelves** | **vuelve** | volvemos | volvéis | **vuelven** |

Other o→ue: *costar, contar, encontrar, mostrar, mover, recordar, sonar, volar*

### E → I (-IR verbs only)

| Verb | Yo | Tú | Él | Nos | Vos | Ellos |
|------|----|----|----|----|-----|-------|
| *pedir* (to ask for) | **pido** | **pides** | **pide** | pedimos | pedís | **piden** |
| *decir* (to say/tell) | **digo** | **dices** | **dice** | decimos | decís | **dicen** |
| *seguir* (to follow/continue) | **sigo** | **sigues** | **sigue** | seguimos | seguís | **siguen** |

Other e→i: *conseguir, elegir, reír, repetir, servir, vestir*

### U → UE (one common verb)

*jugar* (to play): **juego, juegas, juega, jugamos, jugáis, juegan**

## 📚 4. Key Irregular Verbs — Complete Present Conjugations

Memorize these completely. They appear in nearly every utterance.

### SER (to be — identity, permanent characteristics)

| yo | tú | él/ella | nosotros | vosotros | ellos |
|----|----|----|---------|---------|-------|
| soy | eres | es | somos | sois | son |

### ESTAR (to be — states, conditions, location)

| yo | tú | él/ella | nosotros | vosotros | ellos |
|----|----|----|---------|---------|-------|
| estoy | estás | está | estamos | estáis | están |

### IR (to go)

| yo | tú | él/ella | nosotros | vosotros | ellos |
|----|----|----|---------|---------|-------|
| voy | vas | va | vamos | vais | van |

### TENER (to have — possession + idioms)

| yo | tú | él/ella | nosotros | vosotros | ellos |
|----|----|----|---------|---------|-------|
| tengo | tienes | tiene | tenemos | tenéis | tienen |

### HACER (to do/make)

| yo | tú | él/ella | nosotros | vosotros | ellos |
|----|----|----|---------|---------|-------|
| hago | haces | hace | hacemos | hacéis | hacen |

### SABER (to know — facts/how to do) | CONOCER (to know — people/places)

| | yo | tú | él | nos | vos | ellos |
|----|----|----|----|----|-----|-------|
| *saber* | sé | sabes | sabe | sabemos | sabéis | saben |
| *conocer* | **conozco** | conoces | conoce | conocemos | conocéis | conocen |

## 📚 5. SER vs. ESTAR — The Most Important Distinction in Spanish

Both translate to "to be" — but they are not interchangeable. The distinction is systematic.

### SER — Use for:

| Category | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **Identity** — who/what something IS | *Soy ingeniero. Ella es médica.* |
| **Nationality and origin** | *Soy americano. Es de México.* |
| **Permanent characteristics** | *El cielo es azul. La miel es dulce.* |
| **Material/composition** | *La mesa es de madera.* |
| **Ownership** | *El libro es de María.* |
| **Time** (dates, days, hours) | *Son las tres. Hoy es lunes.* |
| **Passive voice** (with past participle) | *El puente fue construido en 1900.* |
| **Event location** (where an event takes place) | *La reunión es en la sala.* |

### ESTAR — Use for:

| Category | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **Location** of people/things | *Estoy en casa. El libro está en la mesa.* |
| **Temporary states** | *Estoy cansado. Está enferma.* |
| **Emotions and feelings** | *Estoy feliz. Está enojado.* |
| **Progressive tenses** (estar + gerund) | *Estoy trabajando.* |
| **Results of actions** | *La ventana está abierta.* (result of being opened) |
| **Marital status** (in practice) | *Estoy casado.* |

### The adjective flip — SER vs. ESTAR changes meaning

Several adjectives change meaning depending on which verb they follow:

| Adjective | With SER | With ESTAR |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| *aburrido* | He is boring (personality) | He is bored (feeling right now) |
| *listo* | She is clever (character) | She is ready (state) |
| *malo* | He is bad/evil (character) | He is sick (state) |
| *bueno* | She is good/kind (character) | She is tasty/well (state) |
| *vivo* | He is clever/sharp (character) | He is alive (state) |
| *seguro* | It is safe (characteristic) | I am sure (state of certainty) |
| *muerto* | It was always dead/dead by nature | He is dead (result of dying) |

## 📚 6. Forming Questions and Negations

### Questions
Spanish questions are formed by **inverting word order** (subject after verb) OR simply by using rising intonation. There is **no do-support** (no equivalent of "do you…?").

*¿Hablas español?* (Do you speak Spanish?)
*¿Dónde vives?* (Where do you live?)
*¿Qué hace ella?* (What does she do?)

**Question words:**
*¿Qué?* (What?) | *¿Quién/Quiénes?* (Who?) | *¿Cuándo?* (When?) | *¿Dónde?* (Where?) | *¿Por qué?* (Why?) | *¿Cómo?* (How?) | *¿Cuánto/a/os/as?* (How much/many?) | *¿Cuál/Cuáles?* (Which?)

### Negations
**No** before the verb. That's it.

*No hablo alemán.* (I don't speak German.)
*No sé dónde está.* (I don't know where he is.)

**Double negation is required** when a negative word follows the verb:
*No tengo **nada**.* (I don't have anything. / I have nothing.)
*No hay **nadie** aquí.* (There is nobody here.)
*No voy **nunca**.* (I never go.)

Negative words: *nada* (nothing), *nadie* (nobody), *nunca/jamás* (never), *ningún/ninguno* (none/no)

## ⚠️ 7. Common Errors

| Error | Wrong | Right |
|-------|-------|-------|
| SER for location | *La tienda es aquí.* | *La tienda **está** aquí.* |
| ESTAR for identity | *Estoy americano.* | ***Soy** americano.* |
| Forgetting stem change | *Yo poder ir.* | *Yo **puedo** ir.* |
| Adding subject pronoun unnecessarily | *Yo hablo mucho.* (unless emphatic) | *Hablo mucho.* |
| Do-support | *¿Do hablas?* (impossible) | *¿Hablas?* |
