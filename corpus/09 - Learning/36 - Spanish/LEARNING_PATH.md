# 🛤️ Spanish — Learning Path

> This document is **hand-curated** and should remain stable.

---

## 📋 Prerequisites

Before starting this track, you should have:

1. **No prior Spanish required** — the track starts at zero.
2. **English grammar foundation** — knowing what a verb, noun, subject, and object are dramatically speeds up the grammar chapters. See [[38 - English/Subject_Plan|Track 19 English]] if needed.
3. **A spaced-repetition tool** — Anki (free) set up and ready. The vocabulary load requires SR.
4. **Headphones** — pronunciation and listening practice are core, not optional.

---

## 📖 Recommended Chapter Order

### Phase 1: Foundation — A2 Level (Weeks 1–6)

| Order | Chapter | Rationale |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | [[36.1 - Phonetics, Pronunciation & Alphabet]] | Start with sound — wrong pronunciation habits are very hard to undo later |
| 2 | [[36.2 - Nouns, Articles & Gender]] | The type system of Spanish — everything else requires it |
| 3 | [[36.3 - Pronouns & Object Placement]] | Pronouns are everywhere; placement rules are unlike English |
| 4 | [[36.4 - Verb System & Present Tense]] | The engine of the language; reach conversational present-tense before moving on |

**Phase 1 deliverable:** Hold a 5-minute present-tense conversation on a familiar topic. Introduce yourself, describe your work, ask basic questions.

### Phase 2: Past & Future — B1 Level (Weeks 7–16)

| Order | Chapter | Rationale |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| 5 | [[36.5 - Past Tenses]] | Preterite + imperfect together — the most important grammar chapter |
| 6 | [[36.6 - Future, Conditional & The Subjunctive]] | The subjunctive is the hardest chapter; the future/conditional are prerequisites |

**Phase 2 deliverable:** Narrate a story in the past. Express hypotheticals and wishes. Navigate a restaurant, hotel, or travel scenario end-to-end in Spanish.

### Phase 3: Fluency — B2/C1 Level (Weeks 17–26)

| Order | Chapter | Rationale |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| 7 | [[36.7 - Idioms, Expressions & Regional Variation]] | The difference between sounding correct and sounding like a person |
| 8 | [[36.8 - Reading, Listening & Cultural Immersion]] | Transition from structured learning to native media immersion |

**Phase 3 deliverable:** Watch a 20-minute Dreaming Spanish video at "Advanced" level and understand ≥80%. Read a news article on BBC Mundo with a dictionary for ≤10% of words.

## 🔀 What to Do Every Single Day (Parallel Habits)

| Daily habit | Time | Why |
|-------------|------|-----|
| Anki vocabulary review | 15–20 min | SR vocabulary is non-negotiable |
| Dreaming Spanish video | 20–30 min | Comprehensible input builds grammar implicitly |
| Language Transfer episode | 20 min (Phase 1–2 only) | Active grammar derivation |
| Read one BBC Mundo headline | 5 min (Phase 2+) | Real Spanish in context |

> **The immersion law:** At any given phase, the activity with the highest return on time is comprehensible input just above your level. Everything else accelerates it — nothing replaces it.

## ➡️ What Comes Next

After completing this Spanish track:

1. **Native media consumption** — switch to Spanish-only content for entertainment where possible (Netflix + Language Reactor, Spanish podcasts, Spanish YouTube in your areas of interest).
2. **Language exchange** — HelloTalk or Tandem; use the speaking skills built in this track.
3. **Portuguese** — after Spanish B2, Brazilian Portuguese is achievable in ~3 months due to ~70% lexical overlap.
4. **Italian** — similar path; Spanish opens the Romance language family.

---

## 🚫 What This Track Does NOT Cover

- **Business Spanish** (specific genres: legal, medical, financial) — built on top of this foundation.
- **Writing at C1+ level** — this track targets spoken fluency and reading comprehension; advanced composition is a separate study.
- **Formal grammar drilling beyond the covered topics** — the goal is communication, not perfect grammar test scores.
