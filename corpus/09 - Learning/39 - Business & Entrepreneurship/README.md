## 🔭 2026 Industry Snapshot

| Area | 2026 Reality | Primary sources |
|---|---|---|
| AI-native SaaS | Solo builders reaching $1M ARR in under 12 months is no longer anomalous; AI tooling has compressed GTM cycles drastically. | a16z State of AI 2025, YC W2026 |
| Bootstrapping vs VC | The 2022–2024 VC winter pushed many founders toward revenue-first, capital-efficient models. Bootstrapping is no longer second-tier. | [indiehackers.com](https://www.indiehackers.com/) |
| Pricing as moat | SaaS companies using value-based pricing with segmented tiers grow 2–3× faster than cost-plus pricing companies, per ProfitWell research. | [profitwell.com/recur/blog](https://www.profitwell.com/recur/blog/saas-pricing-strategies) |
| PLG maturity | PLG + sales-assist (PQLs handed to AEs) is the 2026 dominant motion for developer tools and horizontal SaaS. | [openviewpartners.com](https://openviewpartners.com/) |
| SAFE dominance | Post-money YC SAFEs are the near-universal instrument for US early-stage fundraising. Convertible notes declining except as bridge vehicles. | [ycombinator.com/resources/safe-primer](https://www.ycombinator.com/resources/safe-primer) |
| Solo founder legitimacy | YC data shows solo founders have higher success rates in AI/dev tools categories; AI coding tools compress team size requirements. | [ycombinator.com](https://www.ycombinator.com/) |
| Vibe coding + sales | The 2026 solo founder archetype: builds with AI-assisted coding, launches PLG, does first 10–20 sales calls themselves. | [firstround.com/review](https://review.firstround.com/) |
