# 13.6 HCI Heuristics — Applied to Bio-Tracking Apps

#review/biomech

---

## Nielsen's 10 Heuristics — Bio-Tracking Application Audit

### Heuristic 1: Visibility of System Status

**Principle:** The system should always keep users informed about what is going on, through appropriate feedback within reasonable time.

**Bio-Tracking Application:**
- ✅ Show BLE connection status (connected/searching/disconnected) with icon + color
- ✅ Display recording state prominently (pulsing red dot = recording)
- ✅ Show data freshness ("Last HR update: 2s ago")
- ✅ Battery level of external sensor
- ❌ Don't: silently lose connection and show stale data

**Fitts's Law Consideration:** Status indicators should be in peripheral vision (top bar) — small but always visible. No interaction needed, so size is less critical than position.

---

### Heuristic 2: Match Between System and Real World

**Principle:** The system should speak the users' language, with words, phrases, and concepts familiar to the user.

**Bio-Tracking Application:**
- ✅ "Heart Rate" not "Cardiac Frequency"
- ✅ "Recovery Score" not "RMSSD normalized parasympathetic index"
- ✅ Use BPM (beats per minute), not Hz
- ✅ Show HR zones as colors matching universal conventions (blue=easy, red=max)
- ✅ For advanced users: option to show raw metrics (RMSSD, SDNN, LF/HF)

---

### Heuristic 3: User Control and Freedom

**Principle:** Users often choose system functions by mistake and need a clearly marked "emergency exit."

**Bio-Tracking Application:**
- ✅ "Undo" for accidentally stopping a recording (5-second grace period)
- ✅ "Discard" vs "Save" choice after stopping
- ✅ Back navigation from any detail screen
- ✅ Cancel BLE pairing process
- ❌ Don't: auto-delete recordings without confirmation

---

### Heuristic 4: Consistency and Standards

**Principle:** Users should not have to wonder whether different words, situations, or actions mean the same thing.

**Bio-Tracking Application:**
- ✅ Follow platform conventions (iOS bottom tab bar, Android top app bar)
- ✅ Consistent color coding: red = high HR, blue = recovery, green = optimal
- ✅ Same gesture patterns throughout (swipe left = delete, pull down = refresh)
- ✅ Consistent metric formatting (always "XX BPM", never "XX beats/min" elsewhere)

---

### Heuristic 5: Error Prevention

**Principle:** Even better than good error messages is a careful design which prevents a problem from occurring in the first place.

**Bio-Tracking Application:**
- ✅ Disable "Start" button until BLE sensor is connected
- ✅ Confirm before deleting workout history
- ✅ Auto-save recordings every 30 seconds (prevent data loss on crash)
- ✅ Validate HR data range (reject readings < 20 or > 250 BPM as sensor error)
- ✅ Lock screen prevention during active recording

---

### Heuristic 6: Recognition Rather Than Recall

**Principle:** Minimize the user's memory load by making objects, actions, and options visible.

**Bio-Tracking Application:**
- ✅ Show recent workouts on home screen (not buried in menu)
- ✅ Display last known HR zone when reconnecting
- ✅ Visual HR zone chart (don't make user remember zone boundaries)
- ✅ Show sensor name + icon (not just MAC address)

---

### Heuristic 7: Flexibility and Efficiency of Use

**Principle:** Accelerators — unseen by the novice user — may often speed up the interaction for the expert user.

**Bio-Tracking Application:**
- ✅ Quick-start: one tap to begin recording with last-used settings
- ✅ Customizable dashboard (choose which metrics to display)
- ✅ Gesture shortcuts (double-tap to mark a lap/interval)
- ✅ Apple Watch complication for instant HR glance
- ✅ Export data as CSV/JSON for advanced analysis

---

### Heuristic 8: Aesthetic and Minimalist Design

**Principle:** Dialogues should not contain information which is irrelevant or rarely needed.

**Bio-Tracking Application:**
- ✅ Main screen: HR + HRV + Duration only (3 metrics)
- ✅ Advanced metrics behind one tap (progressive disclosure)
- ✅ Clean typography, generous whitespace
- ✅ Dark mode default (reduces eye strain during exercise, saves OLED battery)
- ❌ Don't: show LF/HF ratio, SDNN, pNN50, VLF power all on the main screen

---

### Heuristic 9: Help Users Recognize, Diagnose, and Recover from Errors

**Principle:** Error messages should be expressed in plain language, precisely indicate the problem, and constructively suggest a solution.

**Bio-Tracking Application:**
- ✅ "Heart rate sensor disconnected. Move closer to your phone or check sensor battery."
- ✅ "Unusual heart rate detected (3 BPM). This may be a sensor error. Check strap contact."
- ✅ "Recording paused — no data for 30 seconds. Resume or save partial recording?"
- ❌ Don't: "Error code 0x4F: BLE GATT characteristic read failed"

---

### Heuristic 10: Help and Documentation

**Principle:** It may be necessary to provide help and documentation. Any such information should be easy to search, focused on the user's task, list concrete steps, and not be too large.

**Bio-Tracking Application:**
- ✅ Contextual tooltips: tap "RMSSD" label → "Root Mean Square of Successive Differences. Higher = better recovery. Your baseline: 112 ms."
- ✅ Onboarding flow: 3-screen tutorial on first launch
- ✅ In-app FAQ searchable by keyword
- ✅ Link to detailed HRV interpretation guide

## Fitts's Law — Applied Calculations for Bio-Tracking UI

### Touch Target Sizing Table

| Element | Distance (px) | Width (px) | ID (bits) | MT (ms) | Verdict |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Start button (thumb zone) | 80 | 88 | 1.25 | 238 | ✅ Excellent |
| Stop button (during exercise) | 120 | 60 | 2.00 | 350 | ✅ Good |
| Settings gear icon | 300 | 44 | 3.27 | 541 | ⚠️ Acceptable |
| Small chart data point | 150 | 20 | 3.17 | 526 | ❌ Too small for exercise |
| Bottom nav tab | 40 | 70 | 1.03 | 205 | ✅ Excellent |

### Fitts's Law Design Rules for Exercise Apps

1. **Primary actions (Start/Stop):** W ≥ 80px, positioned in thumb zone (D < 100px)
2. **Secondary actions (Lap, Mark):** W ≥ 60px
3. **Navigation:** W ≥ 48px (platform minimum)
4. **Data points (interactive charts):** W ≥ 30px (only for post-exercise review)
5. **During exercise:** Increase all targets by 30% (reduced motor precision at high HR)

### The "Edge Effect" (Infinite Width)

Buttons placed at screen edges have effectively infinite width in one direction (the finger can't overshoot). This makes edge-positioned elements faster to acquire:

$$
ID_{\text{edge}} = \log_2\left(\frac{2D}{W_{\text{effective}}}\right) \approx \log_2\left(\frac{2D}{\infty}\right) \rightarrow 0
$$

**Application:** Place "Stop Recording" at the very bottom edge of the screen for fastest possible acquisition during exercise.

## Cognitive Walkthrough Template — Bio-Tracking App

For each user task, answer:
1. Will the user try to achieve the right effect? (Goal formation)
2. Will the user notice that the correct action is available? (Action visibility)
3. Will the user associate the correct action with the desired effect? (Label clarity)
4. If the correct action is performed, will the user see progress? (Feedback)

### Example: "Start recording a workout"

| Step | Action | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | Open app | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ (app opens to main screen) |
| 2 | Verify sensor connected | ✅ | ✅ (BLE icon) | ✅ | ✅ (green = connected) |
| 3 | Tap "Start" | ✅ | ✅ (large, prominent) | ✅ (labeled "Start") | ✅ (timer begins, dot pulses) |
