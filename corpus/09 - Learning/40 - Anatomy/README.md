## The Body-Systems Method

Anatomy is studied **twice** in this vault — once by **system** (chapters 40.2–40.7) and once by **region** (chapter 40.8). The system pass builds the conceptual model; the regional pass is how anatomy is actually used clinically. Cross-links connect every structure to its system note.

## Target Milestones

- **Chapters 40.1–40.3:** Skeletal + muscular literacy (name, locate, and describe function of all major bones and muscles)
- **Chapters 40.4–40.5:** Neuro + cardiovascular literacy (trace neural pathways and vascular circuits)
- **Chapters 40.6–40.8:** Full-body systems + regional clinical application
