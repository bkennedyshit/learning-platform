```bash
cd "C:/Obsidian Vault/Bill's Vault/05-Knowledge_Foundation/09 - Learning/07 - Math and Physics/12 - Solid Mechanics & Materials Science"
python "_practice/scripts/<chapter>_<topic>.py" --count 8 --seed 42
```

view, do the problems on paper, click **Show solution** to verify.
