# Track 06 — Game Design Theory: Learning Path

> *"Design is not just what it looks like and feels like. Design is how it works."*
> — **Steve Jobs** (applicable to games as much as products)

---

## 🎯 Path Overview

**Duration:** ~12 weeks at 2 hours/day
**Prerequisites:** None (theory track — no code required)
**Parallel tracks (recommended):**
- [[Track 04 Game_Dev]] — implement what you design (engineering)
- [[Track 09 VR & 3D]] — build in engines (Unity/Godot/Unreal)

**Outcome:** You will be able to design, document, balance, and iterate on game systems from first principles — whether for a solo indie project, a BMX game concept, a VR experience, or a content-creation gamification system.

## 📅 Weekly Schedule

### Phase 1: Foundations (Weeks 1–4)

| Week | Chapter | Focus | Companion Reading |
|------|---------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | [[25.1 - Player Psychology & Motivation - The MDA Framework]] | Why games are fun; MDA; Flow | Koster Ch. 1–4, GMTK "What Makes a Good Game?" |
| 2 | [[25.2 - Core Mechanics & Systems Design]] | Verbs, resources, loops | Schell Lenses 1–20, Cook "Loops and Arcs" |
| 3 | [[25.3 - Dynamics, Balance & Feedback Loops]] | Emergence, balance, fairness | Sirlin "Playing to Win" Ch. 1–5 |
| 4 | Review + Design Exercise | Design a complete mechanic set for a BMX trick game | Combine 6.1–25.3 into one design doc |

### Phase 2: Craft (Weeks 5–8)

| Week | Chapter | Focus | Companion Reading |
|------|---------|-------|-------------------|
| 5 | [[25.4 - Level Design & Spatial Pacing]] | Teaching through space; pacing curves | GMTK "Zelda Dungeon Design", Totten |
| 6 | [[25.5 - Narrative & Worldbuilding]] | Story structures; emergent narrative | Adams "Fundamentals of Game Design" Ch. 7 |
| 7 | [[25.6 - Aesthetics, Juice & Game Feel]] | Polish, feel, the 50ms rule | Swink "Game Feel", "Juice It or Lose It" talk |
| 8 | Review + Prototype | Paper-prototype a level with narrative beats | Combine 6.4–25.6 into playable concept |

### Phase 3: Professional Practice (Weeks 9–12)

| Week | Chapter | Focus | Companion Reading |
|------|---------|-------|-------------------|
| 9 | [[25.7 - Playtesting & Iteration]] | Scientific testing; observation protocols | Fullerton Ch. 8–9, GDC playtest talks |
| 10 | [[25.8 - Monetization & Live Ops]] | Ethical business models; retention | GDC "F2P Design" talks, Sirlin on fairness |
| 11 | Capstone Project Part 1 | Full GDD for a personal game concept | Apply all 8 chapters |
| 12 | Capstone Project Part 2 | Playtest plan + balance spreadsheet + pitch deck | Portfolio-ready deliverable |

## 📖 Daily Study Structure (2 hours)

| Block | Duration | Activity |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Theory | 40 min | Read chapter section + take notes |
| Video | 20 min | Watch one GMTK/GDC/Sakurai video on the topic |
| Exercise | 40 min | Complete one design exercise from the chapter |
| Reflection | 20 min | Journal: "What game I played uses this? How would I redesign it?" |

## 💡 Tips for Bill

- **BMX connection:** Every chapter has design parallels to trick-based games. Use your rider's intuition about flow, risk/reward, and style expression.
- **Content creator angle:** Game design thinking applies directly to video pacing, audience retention curves, and community engagement.
- **VR focus:** Chapters 25.4 and 25.6 are especially relevant to your VR development goals.
- **Don't just read — design.** Every chapter has exercises. Do them. Bad designs teach more than good theory.
