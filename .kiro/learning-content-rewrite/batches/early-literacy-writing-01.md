- **WWC, Foundational Skills K-3 (2016, rev. 2019), landing page + full PDF:** four recommendations; words → syllables (chin/finger counting) → onset-rime → phonemes; two- or three-phoneme starter words; Elkonin boxes with continuous-sound words (*fan*; f, h, l, m, n, r, s, v, z); sequence of consonants/short vowels, then blends (teach each sound) and digraphs (a single sound, taught as a unit), then silent *e*, then vowel teams; sounding out vs. chunking with *hat*; /mmmaaannn/; minimize the "uh"/schwa after stop sounds; check that a blended word is real; regular vs. irregular high-frequency words, irregular taught as whole words, *of* and *was* as examples; discouraging guessing from first letters or pictures; compound words (*bookshelf*, *strawberry*, *cowboy*); prefixes, suffixes, roots; Example 3.3 definitions of digraphs, blends, vowel teams, diphthongs (oi, ou), r-controlled vowels (ar, er, ir, or, ur), long-*a*/long-*e* spellings; Recommendation 4 modeling expression and phrasing, punctuation, asking questions after reading.
